📌 Watch how
Mateo’s draft
6th Grade Scholar

Although I once argued that the band’s code falls short I now think the code is enough. It is true that the band’s own rules let it bring a traveler to dinner whether he will or no. But the oath also tells the band to help any woman who needs its protection, and in Chapter 14 that promise shows Robin exactly what to do. When the bride comes into the church, she is pale and her eyes are red with weeping. The Bishop of Peterborough is her legal guardian, so the law gives him the say. Robin asks her, “Is he your own choice?” and she answers, “No.” Because the oath tells him to protect her, Robin does not have to make up a rule on the spot. He calls his men, and Allin-a-Dale marries the bride he loves. If the code were only about gold, it would not be enough. Since it also protects people who cannot protect themselves, it tells Robin when to stand against an unfair law. That is why I now say the code is enough.
