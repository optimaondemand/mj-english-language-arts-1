📌 Watch how
Owen’s draft
6th Grade Scholar

The code of the forest is not enough. In Chapter 3, the band swears to look after the poor, the needy, the widow, and the orphan, and that oath is a good one. But in Chapter 6, Robin gives a rule that leaves the choosing to the band alone. He tells Little John to wait beside the Great North Road and bring a guest to dinner “whether he will or no.” The traveler has no vote. Robin names a bold baron, a bishop, or an abbot as the guests who will pay best, and he spares an honest yeoman. Yet who decides which traveler is proud and which is honest? Robin and his men decide, and nobody else. A rule that the band writes for itself makes the band both the judge and the one who gains. The code protects the people Robin approves of, but it gives no one a way to say that the band is wrong. A just code needs more than good intentions. It needs someone outside the band to test it.
