Isaac’s parts
6th Grade Scholar
Thesis

Robin Hood is both an outlaw and a hero, because he breaks the law only to protect people the law has forgotten, and in the end the king himself agrees.

One evidence card

Robin tells the palmer that the band has never hurt “any man knowingly who was honest and true” (Ch. 21). This shows that Robin keeps a rule of his own, and the rule protects honest people.

Concession and answer

A reader could say Robin is only an outlaw, because the palmer points out, “Yet you kill the King’s deer” (Ch. 21). But Robin answers that “we were not outlawed lawfully” (Ch. 21), so the law that named him an outlaw was the real wrong.

Conclusion line

A hero is not someone who never breaks a rule. A hero is someone who asks what a rule is for, and what it costs the people who have no power.
