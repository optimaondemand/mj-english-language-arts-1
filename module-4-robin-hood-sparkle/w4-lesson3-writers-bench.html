Hana’s draft
6th Grade Scholar

My feet hurt, and my purse is empty. Nearly five hundred pounds are gone, and so are three good horses. I walk the road toward Nottingham with two men behind me, and I cursed the day I met that butcher. I am a just man. I keep the King's law, and the law says outlaws must be caught. That is why I went. This morning I rode into Sherwood beside a butcher I did not know, because he said he knew where Robin Hood hides. I was afraid, and I will not pretend I was not. Every step deeper into the trees made me quieter. Still I went on, because I wanted that outlaw more than I wanted to be safe. Now I see how I was fooled. The man sat at my own table and ate my supper, and I never knew him. I shook my fist and swore I would come back with a great force. I meant it. I will not be laughed at in my own town, and the next time I ride into Sherwood, I will not ride alone.
