Where run-ons hide in your own writing
Professor Lovelore At the board
0:29
An AI-generated character and voice.
The word “then”
I fell in the stream then I laughed.→I fell in the stream, and then I laughed.
The comma splice
The bridge was narrow, I would not step aside.→The bridge was narrow, so I would not step aside.
The and-and-and chain
I ran and I jumped and I swam and I laughed.→I ran and I jumped. I swam and I laughed.

Three lenses for your monologue
  1. First Person. Every I, me, and my is marked. A he or Robin that slipped in is flagged.
  2. Senses. What Robin sees, hears, smells, and touches.
  3. Joins. Your compound sentences are marked. A sentence that might be a run-on is flagged.
