🔤 Words: The Tournament and the Glade
Five words from these chapters. Tap a card to hear it.
oppress
To treat people cruelly and unfairly by using power over them.
“robbed innocent men or oppressed them” Robin, Chapter 21
tournament
A public contest where knights compete in jousting and combat.
“A great tournament was being held there by Prince John” Green, Chapter 20
Crusade
One of the medieval wars to control the Holy Land.
“I have fought in the Holy Land with King Richard, and the Crusade was …” The palmer, Chapter 21
pardon
Official forgiveness of a crime that cancels its punishment.
“I freely pardon you, and all here present” King Richard, Chapter 21
waylay
To lie in wait for and stop someone on the road.
“to waylay him in Sherwood” Green, Chapter 20

Isaac’s plan
6th Grade Scholar

Claim: Robin is both: an outlaw by the law, a hero by his choices.

Evidence for: early, he lends Sir Richard the money to keep his land (Chapter 6) · middle, he sends the Butcher’s cart back (Chapter 11) · late, he tells the palmer the band never hurts an honest man, and he protects Marian (Chapter 21).

Evidence against: he and his band kill the King’s deer, and the palmer says so (Chapter 21).

My answer to it: Robin says they were not outlawed lawfully, so the law that named him was the real wrong.

Verdict: Both, and the hero side weighs more: in Chapter 21 the king himself pardons him.
