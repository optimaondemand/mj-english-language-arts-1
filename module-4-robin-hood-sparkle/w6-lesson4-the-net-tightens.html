Professor Lovelore At the board

An AI-generated character and voice.
The missing commas
Friar Tuck a cheerful friar sang the loudest.→Friar Tuck, a cheerful friar, sang the loudest.
At the end of a sentence
Robin welcomed Allin-a-Dale a minstrel.→Robin welcomed Allin-a-Dale, a minstrel.
The check
Can I lift the phrase out and still have a whole sentence?→Then it is an appositive. The commas stay.

Four lenses for your outline
  1. Thesis. Your first sentence names Robin Hood and says “because” or “although.”
  2. Claims. Each body paragraph has an arguable claim, a named scene, and its chapter.
  3. Concession. The strongest objection, and one sentence that answers it.
  4. Appositives. At least two, each set off with commas.
Essential question: What makes a true hero, and what happens when justice and law are not the same thing?
📜 Before and after

Pick your weakest claim and make it better. Show it the way it was, and the way it is now.
