🔤 Words: The Road and the Castle
Four words from these chapters. Tap a card to hear it.
toll
A payment charged for passing along a road or over a bridge.
“he has never paid any toll to us!” Robin, Chapter 11
ambush
A surprise from a hiding place.
“fell upon him from an ambush” Green, Chapter 10
ransom
Money paid to free a prisoner.
“Prince John makes no effort to find and ransom him” Robin, Chapter 11
moat
A wide ditch of water around a castle.
“with towers and a good moat of water!” Much, Chapter 10
