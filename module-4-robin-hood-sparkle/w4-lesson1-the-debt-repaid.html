Professor Lovelore

Well done. Look at what your three answers share. A knight is late because he stopped to help a stranger. The Abbot’s own money ends up repaying the debt that the Abbot caused. A prince’s free feast does not win him the crowd he hoped for. Each time, what happens flips what someone expected.

That flip is called irony, and it is the word for this chapter.
