Direct and indirect characterization
DirectThe narrator tells you what a person is like.
IndirectYou work it out from what a person says and does.
🗣️ The narrator tells
“Robin greeted the knight full courteously.”Green, Chapter 6
👁️ The action shows
“If you have indeed no more,” said Robin, “I will not touch a single penny of it – and if you have need of more, why more will I lend to you.”Green, Chapter 6
Do you learn more about a person from what they say about themselves, or from what they do?

🔤 Words: Hermits, Alms, and a Frugal Diet
Four words from these chapters. Tap a card to hear it.
hermit
A person who lives alone, often for religious reasons.
“He said he would live a hermit in some cell by the river” Marian, Chapter 8
hermitage
The dwelling where a hermit lives.
“the hermitage built against the cliff edge” Green, Chapter 8
alms
Money or gifts given to the poor.
“I do but ask for alms – as is the Church’s due!” Friar Tuck, Chapter 8
frugal
Careful to use very little; plain and simple, not wasteful.
“the frugal diet of a poor hermit vowed to fasting and prayer” Friar Tuck, Chapter 8
