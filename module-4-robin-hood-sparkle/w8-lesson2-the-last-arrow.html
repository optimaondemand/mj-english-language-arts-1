🔤 Words: A Nunnery, a Veil, a Boon, an Heir
Four words from these chapters. Tap a card to hear it.
nunnery
A place where nuns live and work.
“even he dared not tear a nun from a nunnery” Green, Chapter 24
veil
A cloth a nun wears over her head. To “take the veil” is to become a nun.
“take the veil and become one of our sisterhood” The Prioress, Chapter 24
boon
A favour that is asked for.
“Grant me one last boon!” Little John, Chapter 24
heir
A person who will receive someone’s property or title when that person dies.
A king’s oldest child is often the heir to the throne. An example
