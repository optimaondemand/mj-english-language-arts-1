Professor Lovelore

Well done. Look at where your three answers lead. Robin’s anger sends him off to cut a wand, and that gives the stranger his chance to drop the mask. When Gisborne is defeated, Robin uses his enemy’s hood and horn. Then help arrives from nowhere. All of Chapter 19 leans toward the moment the two men face each other, when the tension is highest and the story turns.

That moment is called the climax, and it is the word for this lesson.

📖 Read like a Reader

Robin says this to Little John just before they part. What does it tell you about how Robin sees his place as the leader?

✏️ Read like a Writer

Green sets my men go first against I follow after them inside one sentence. What does putting the two side by side add that a shorter sentence would not?
