Three lenses for your monologue
  1. Voice. Every I, me, and my is marked. A he, his, or a name that slipped in for your speaker is flagged.
  2. Tense. Past and present verbs are marked. A sentence that mixes them is flagged.
  3. His reasons. What he wanted, what he believes about himself, and why he went on.

The question

In Chapter 6 Robin lends Sir Richard four hundred pounds from the band’s own treasury. In Chapter 9 his men search the Cellarer of St Mary’s and find eight hundred pounds in gold meant for Prince John. Robin says the debt has been paid and keeps the gold “for King Richard.” Is that justice, or is it theft with a good reason attached?
