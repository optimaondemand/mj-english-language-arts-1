🧍 Characterization

How a writer shows you who a person is.

📏 A code

A rule a person lives by. What someone will do, and will never do, shows who he is.

🔎 A rule to watch in Chapter 6

Robin tells Little John whom the band may stop on the road, and whom to leave alone.

Well done. Look at what your three answers share. Robin keeps a rule about whom to spare. A Prior will not speak for a kneeling debtor. Two archers shoot as equals and hide who they are. In each one, a person shows you who he is by what he does.

That is characterization: the way a writer lets you know a person. A code, the rule someone lives by, is one of the clearest ways.
