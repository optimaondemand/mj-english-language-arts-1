The comma after a starter clause
Professor Lovelore At the board
0:37
An AI-generated character and voice.
The missing comma
When Robin flung off the beggar’s coat he called out his own name.→When Robin flung off the beggar’s coat, he called out his own name.
The starter word second
Robin called out his own name, when he flung off the beggar’s coat.→Robin called out his own name when he flung off the beggar’s coat.
The check
Does the sentence open with a starter word?→Then the comma goes where that clause ends.

Four lenses for your paragraph
  1. Concession. Your first sentence is checked: does it begin with Although or Even though, and does it state your earlier position fairly?
  2. Opposite. The words that turn the paragraph around are marked, so you can see where the new claim begins.
  3. Scene. The names and chapters that show your scene are marked, and so are the words that tell why it supports your claim.
  4. Complex sentences. Every starter word is marked, and an opening clause with no comma is flagged. You need at least three.
150 to 200 words · one paragraph · at least three complex sentences
📜 Before and after

Take your opening sentence and make it better. Show it the way it was, and the way it is now.
