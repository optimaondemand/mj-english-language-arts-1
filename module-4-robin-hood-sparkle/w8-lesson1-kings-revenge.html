🍂 Falling action

What winds down after the high point, while the story keeps moving.

One sentence from the chapter, three ways.
‘I forget nothing,’ said Little John doggedly.Green, Chapter 22
📖 Read like a Reader

Robin has just laughed at Little John’s warnings. How does Little John answer, and what does that show about how he feels about the danger?

✏️ Read like a Writer

Green puts Little John’s words first and the speaker after them, and ends with the single word doggedly. What does that word add that the words alone do not?
