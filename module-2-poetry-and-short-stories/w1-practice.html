This is optional extra practice. Nothing here is graded. Use it if you want more reps on this week's grammar and vocabulary: common and proper nouns, the title rule, and three words from "The Tale of Custard the Dragon."
⚡ GRAMMAR PRACTICE · NOUNS AND TITLES
Choose the best answer for each. Pick one and you'll get instant feedback.
1. Which word is a proper noun in this sentence: "My dog Custard hid under the porch."?
2. What's wrong with this sentence: "belinda scolded her cat for boasting."?
3. Which title is punctuated correctly?

⚡ TAP TO REVEAL

Mock means to make fun of someone. That's what the other animals do to the dragon.

Boast means to talk with too much pride about yourself, exactly what the loud little pets keep doing.
