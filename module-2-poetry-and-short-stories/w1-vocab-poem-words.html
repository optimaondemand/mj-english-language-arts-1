Lesson 2.06
Words from "Custard," and How to Make a Sentence Your Own
Grammar & Vocabulary · Week 1
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
The best way to learn a word is to use it three different ways.
You'll meet a few words that do real work in the poem, then practice the move you'll use all module: take a sentence with the word and rewrite it three ways: swap a synonym, stretch it longer, or imitate its shape with new content.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to explain three words from the poem in my own words.
2
I will be able to take a sentence and rewrite it three ways to make a word my own.
⚡ WARM-UP · WHERE DO YOU LAND?
How well do you already know the word cowardly? (No wrong answer. Just check in.)
📚 THE LESSON

A few words carry a lot of weight in this poem, and they are worth owning. Cowardly means showing fear and avoiding danger; it is the word everyone wrongly pins on Custard. Mock means to make fun of someone, which is what the other animals do to the dragon. And boast means to talk with too much pride about yourself, exactly what the loud little pets keep doing. You don't really learn a word by memorizing a definition; you learn it by using it. So here is the move you'll practice every week: take a sentence that contains the word, then rewrite it three different ways: swap in a word that means almost the same thing, stretch the sentence with more detail, or keep the sentence's shape but fill it with something from your own life. Each rewrite forces you to handle the word a little differently, and that is what makes it stick.

💡 WORD WORK IN YOUR READING JOURNAL
Start with this sentence: "The other animals mocked the cowardly dragon." Rewrite it three ways: (1) swap a synonym for mocked or cowardly; (2) stretch it with more detail about how they mocked him; (3) keep its shape but write about a time someone was teased in your own world. Add one line: which rewrite taught you the word best, and why?
⚡ CHECK YOURSELF · USING THE WORDS
Choose the best answer for each. Pick one and you'll get instant feedback.
1. The little pets keep telling everyone how brave they are. The best word for this is…
2. Which word is the closest synonym for cowardly?
3. Which is a good rewrite of "The animals mocked the dragon" using your own life?
