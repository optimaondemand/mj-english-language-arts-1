Grammar & Vocabulary · Week 2
📖 BEFORE YOU START
This is optional extra practice. Nothing here is graded. Use it if you want more reps on this week's grammar and vocabulary: action and linking verbs, apostrophes, and three words from "Do Not Go Gentle into That Good Night."
⚡ GRAMMAR PRACTICE · IS IT CORRECT?
Read each sentence and decide how it uses this week's grammar rule. Pick one and you'll get instant feedback.
1. "The old man's cane fell to the floor."
2. "Its going to rage all night."
3. "The fire burns wild." Is 'burns' an action verb or a linking verb?
📚 VOCABULARY PRACTICE · WORDS FOR STRONG FEELING
Before you open each drop-down, say your own definition out loud or in your head. Then open it and compare.
What does rage mean? Guess first, then open.
Rage means to feel or show violent, uncontrolled anger. It is the poem's most important verb, the opposite of going "gentle."
What does grieve mean? Guess first, then open.
Grieve means to feel deep sadness, especially over a loss. It is the feeling underneath all the fierceness in the poem.
What does figurative mean? Guess first, then open.
Figurative describes language that means something other than its plain, literal sense. It is the tool that lets "night" stand for death.
