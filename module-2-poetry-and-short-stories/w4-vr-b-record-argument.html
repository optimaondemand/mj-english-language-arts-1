⚠️ Solo and Self-Paced · No Live Session
This activity replaces what used to be a live, scheduled debate. You complete it on your own, whenever you are ready. Record your argument alone. Nothing here requires you to be online at the same time as anyone else.
📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Say It Out Loud Before You Write It
The spoken rehearsal of your project letter
Speaking an argument out loud tests whether it holds together. You will record a short spoken version of your letter to the author you chose: first state the strongest, fairest version of the author's claim, then answer it with your counter-claim and one piece of evidence, and close with what you think the text really teaches. Then you write the full letter in Word, using what you heard yourself say.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
If the author could answer you, what is their strongest reply, and how do you answer it?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset, or any voice recorder if VR is unavailable
Activity type: solo. No live session, no scheduled time, no classmates present.
Tool you'll use: a voice recorder. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your planned letter from VR 2.4A, with your claim, counter-claim, evidence, and your answer to the strongest objection.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your VR app on your own. Out loud, rehearse once from your plan: state the author's claim fairly, then your counter-claim.
2
Make a voice recording (2–3 minutes). A short screen or video recording also works. In it: (a) state the strongest version of the author's claim; (b) give your counter-claim; (c) support it with one specific moment from the text; (d) answer the best objection the author could raise; (e) close by naming what you think the text is really teaching.
3
Listen back once. Note the one spot that sounded weakest out loud. That is the part of your letter to strengthen when you write the draft.
4
Save your recording. Submit it to the Canvas assignment VR 2.4B: Record Your Argument with a one-sentence note on what you found hardest to say out loud. Then write your full letter in Word for the module project.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My recording (2–3 minutes) states the opposing view fairly before answering it.
☐
I gave my counter-claim, one moment from the text, an answer to the strongest objection, and a closing idea.
☐
I submitted my recording to VR 2.4B with a one-sentence note.
