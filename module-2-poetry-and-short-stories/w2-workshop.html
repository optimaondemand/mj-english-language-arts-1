Week 2 · Workshop Day
Put the Week to Work
Your studio day, with no new reading. You write your own version of the poem, build in VR, and join the week's discussion.
📝 WHAT TODAY IS FOR
You've heard the poem, mapped its returning lines, and unlocked its meaning. Today you make something of your own: a poem that borrows this one's shape, VR work that brainstorms and builds your response to the poem, and your first move in this week's class discussion. Work the three stops below in order; each tells you exactly where to go in Canvas.
⚡ CHECK IN
How do you feel about writing your own version of a poem? (No wrong answer. This helps you pick where to slow down.)
📝 STOP 1 · QUICK WRITE (graded)
This week's Quick Write uses the poem's shape as a frame: you'll keep its repeating lines and the rhyme pattern, then fill the blanks with your own words to make your own version. Write it in Microsoft Word and submit through Canvas. Full directions and the M365 steps are on the Canvas assignment.
➡️ Go to the Week 2 Quick Write [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
🧐 STOP 2 · VR ACTIVITY (completion)
Use VR this week as a thinking tool. In VR 2.2A you brainstorm your response to the poem, and in VR 2.2B you build your case for the "Dear Author" letter. The VR pages have the steps.
🧐 Optima's VR platform is being upgraded. Your facilitator will confirm how to open the activity in the current app.
➡️ Open this week's VR activities [Teacher: link VR 2.2A and 2.2B]
💬 STOP 3 · CLASS DISCUSSION (graded)
Join the Week 2 Discussion: "What Is the Poem Arguing?" Post your one-sentence statement of the poem's message and say whether you agree, with one reason from the text. Then reply to one classmate. This is asynchronous; post and reply on your own schedule during the week.
➡️ Go to the Week 2 Discussion [Teacher: link the Canvas discussion]
📚 ONE NOTE FOR YOUR MODULE PROJECT
In your Module Organizer, add the central message of "Do Not Go Gentle into That Good Night" in one sentence and one honest reaction: do you agree with what it argues? Your end-of-module letter, "Dear Author, I Disagree," will grow from notes like this.
💪 YOU'RE DONE WHEN
You've submitted your own poem, completed your VR activity, posted and replied in the discussion, and added one note to your Module Organizer. Strong week.
