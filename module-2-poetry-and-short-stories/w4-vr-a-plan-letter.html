📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Plan "Dear Author, I Disagree"
Choose one author: Nash, Thomas, or Hawthorne
This week you write the project. First you plan it. A board lets you see the whole letter at once and move pieces around before you commit them to Word. You will lay your letter out in order: the author's claim stated fairly, your counter-claim, your three pieces of evidence (early, middle, late), the best objection and your answer, and your closing idea about what the text really teaches. When the order looks right, writing it is mostly typing.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
In what order will your letter move from the author's claim to your conclusion, and where is each piece of evidence?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place and reorder notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: the "build your case" board for the author you chose (from VR 2.1B, 2.2B, or 2.3B) and your project file.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a board and lay out six labeled spots in a line, in this order: (1) the author's claim, stated fairly; (2) your counter-claim; (3) early evidence; (4) middle evidence; (5) late evidence; (6) your closing idea about what the text really teaches.
2
Drop a note into each spot. Use the work you already built for your chosen author. For your three evidence notes, name one moment from early, one from the middle, and one from late in the text.
3
Add one more note off to the side: the strongest objection the author could raise, and your answer to it. Decide where in the line that note will go when you write.
4
Walk the line once and reorder anything that reads out of place. Make sure the author's claim is stated fairly before your counter-claim, and that your strongest evidence is not buried in the middle.
5
Take a screenshot of your planned letter. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 2.4A: Plan Your Letter, then use it as your outline when you draft in Word. (No headset? Lay the same plan out on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My plan has all six spots filled: the author's claim, my counter-claim, three pieces of evidence (early/middle/late), and my closing idea.
☐
I included a note naming the strongest objection and my answer.
☐
I submitted a screenshot of my plan to VR 2.4A.
