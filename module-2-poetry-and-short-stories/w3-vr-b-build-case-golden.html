📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
The Golden Touch
Nathaniel Hawthorne · building toward your letter
In VR 2.3A you gathered ideas. Now you arrange them into a case. A board is a tool for organizing an argument, the same way an outline is. You will lay out the strongest, fairest version of Hawthorne's point on one side, your own response on the other, with evidence under each. This is the last text you will study before you write, so a strong case here gives you a ready draft if you choose Hawthorne for your project letter.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
If Hawthorne were sitting across from you, what is his strongest point, and what would you say back?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space for two columns of notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your brainstorm board from VR 2.3A and the story.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a board and make two columns: "What Hawthorne is saying" and "What I think."
2
In the first column, write the strongest, fairest version of Hawthorne's claim, with two evidence notes from the story (for example, Marygold turning to gold, and Midas saying a loving heart is worth more than all his wealth). State his point at its best.
3
In the second column, write your counter-point (agree, partly agree, or disagree) and two notes that support it. For example, is the lesson really "have less," or is it "do not love the wrong things"? Midas keeps his gold at the end. He just values it differently.
4
Add one note that names the best reply Hawthorne could give you, and one short note answering it. Knowing his strongest comeback is how you make your letter hold up.
5
Take a screenshot of your two-column case. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 2.3B: Build Your Case. (No headset? Build the same two columns on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board has two columns: Hawthorne's strongest point and my own point, each with two pieces of evidence.
☐
I named the best reply Hawthorne could give and answered it in one note.
☐
I submitted a screenshot of my case to VR 2.3B.
