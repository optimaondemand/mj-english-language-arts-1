Week 4 · Workshop Day
Finish Strong: Revise and Submit Your Letter
This is your studio day, with no new reading. You revise the letter, record your rebuttal, and submit the module project.
📝 WHAT TODAY IS FOR
You have a claim, a fair counter-argument, evidence, and correct quotations. Today you pull it all together into a finished letter and turn it in. Work through the four stops below in order. Each one tells you exactly where to go in Canvas.
⚡ CHECK IN
What does your letter most need before you submit? (No wrong answer. This helps you aim today's revision.)
📝 STOP 1 · QUICK WRITE (graded)
Write this week's Quick Write in Microsoft Word and submit it through Canvas. This week the Quick Write is a targeted revision: choose the single weakest passage in your letter and rewrite it to be clearer or better supported. Turn on Track Changes so your revision is visible. Full directions and the M365 steps are on the Canvas assignment.
➡️ Go to the Week 4 Quick Write [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
🧐 STOP 2 · VR ACTIVITY (completion)
Use VR to brainstorm then build toward your project. This week you record a short spoken rebuttal: say your claim aloud and answer the strongest objection, the way you would defend your letter face to face. The VR page has the full steps.
🧐 Optima's VR platform is being upgraded. Your facilitator will confirm how to open the activity in the current app.
➡️ Open the Week 4 VR Activity: Recorded Rebuttal
📚 STOP 3 · MODULE PROJECT · SUBMIT
Finish and submit your "Dear Author, I Disagree" letter. Make a clean copy of your draft named LastName_FirstName_DearAuthor_ELA6_Final, check it against the success criteria on the assignment (clear claim, fair counter-argument, three textual moments, a correct quotation and paraphrase, transitions), and submit the Word file through the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab. This is the module's main project.
➡️ Open the Module Project assignment [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
📁 STOP 4 · READING JOURNAL CHECK (completion)
Every two weeks you submit your Reading Journal so it stays real. This is the end-of-Week-4 check. Make sure your journal has your Week 3 and Week 4 notes (the Golden Touch events, your theme sentence, your word-bank rewrites, and your project notes), then submit the Word doc through the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab. It is graded for completion.
➡️ Go to the Reading Journal Check [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
💪 YOU'RE DONE WHEN
You've submitted your Quick Write revision, completed your VR rebuttal, turned in your finished letter, and submitted your Reading Journal. That closes out Module 2. Strong work.
