📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Do Not Go Gentle into That Good Night
Dylan Thomas · read from your own copy
Use VR here as a giant whiteboard you can walk around in: a tool for thinking, the way a word processor is a tool for writing. Today you brainstorm what Thomas's poem actually says, then begin forming your own response. The board feeds your module project, the letter "Dear Author, I Disagree."
You are not visiting a ready-made world. You are building a board of your own ideas, using lines and images from the poem.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What is Thomas urging about how to face death, and do you agree?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place notes and simple objects. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your own copy of the poem and your Reading Journal, open to your Week 2 notes.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a blank board (or clear space) in your VR app. This is your workspace.
2
Make a note for the author's apparent message. In one sentence, write what Thomas seems to be urging: do not meet death quietly or meekly. Instead "rage" and "burn" against it, fighting to the end.
3
Make four or five evidence notes, one detail each, from the poem itself: the two repeated lines that keep returning ("Do not go gentle into that good night" and "Rage, rage against the dying of the light"); the light-and-darkness images for life and death; the wise, good, wild, and grave men who each refuse to slip away quietly; and the personal turn at the end to the speaker's dying father. Use the details you actually noticed.
4
Add your own response on one or two notes: do you agree that fighting to the end is the best way, partly agree, or disagree? Why? Place these notes next to the lines that made you think that way. This is the start of your side of the letter.
5
Take a screenshot of your finished board. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 2.2A: Brainstorm Board. (No headset? Build the same board on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board names Thomas's apparent message and has four or five real details from the poem.
☐
I added my own response (agree, partly agree, or disagree) with a reason.
☐
I submitted a screenshot of my board to VR 2.2A.
