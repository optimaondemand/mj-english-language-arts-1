📖 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
St. George and the Dragon
Michael Lotti, Chapters 10–14
Lotti uses fire two ways in Chapters 10–14: the dragon's fire consumes and destroys; the Christians' candles and inner light illuminate and sustain. You will build a composition that captures this dual nature: two groupings of objects, a passage quotation written on a writable surface, and a short voice recording explaining what Lotti's fire imagery does.
🏭 LITERARY & HISTORICAL FRAME
Fire in two traditions at once: in Roman civic life, fire was sacred. The Vestal Virgins kept Rome's eternal flame, and its extinction signaled divine abandonment. In Christian tradition, the Holy Spirit descends as tongues of fire at Pentecost, and candles define the underground liturgy. Lotti's fire imagery draws on both: the dragon's fire and the Christians' candlelight belong to different cosmologies.
Notice–Name–Apply: in your reading you noticed Lotti's fire imagery and named it as symbolic contrast. This activity is the Apply step: you arrange the contrast in space, making your interpretive choice visible.
The virtue under pressure: perseverance is what the Christians show by sustaining their candlelit community under the shadow of the dragon's fire. Your recording should explain not just what the imagery is, but what moral claim it makes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What is the difference between fire that destroys and fire that illuminates, and how does Lotti use that difference to show what Marcellus is caught between?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset
Activity type: solo; you build and record on your own, at your own pace
Tools you'll use: a blank space with room for two groupings, simple objects to arrange, and a writable surface or note tool you can write a short passage on. [Teacher: confirm the build/objects/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
To start: open a blank space. You are not touring a ready-made world; you build both groupings yourself from objects you choose. (If your app has no writable surface, write the passage on a card or in your M365 Reading Journal and keep it in frame.)
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a blank space in your VR app to build in. This is your workspace.
2
Build Grouping A: Destructive Fire. Choose 2–3 objects representing the dragon's fire: destruction, domination, consuming power. Arrange them on one side.
3
Build Grouping B: Sacred Light. Choose 2–3 different objects representing the Christians' candles, inner steadfastness, or the quiet light that endures. Arrange them on the other side.
4
Write one short passage (a sentence or two) from Chapters 10–14 that you think captures Lotti's fire imagery most powerfully, on a writable surface or note tool. Add a signed label beneath it explaining in one sentence what this passage shows about Lotti's use of fire.
5
Make a voice recording (2–3 minutes), or a short screen/video recording if your app supports it, standing in your composition. Move from Grouping A to Grouping B as you speak, and answer the focus question with specific reference to the text.
6
Save your recording (and a screenshot of your composition if you can take one). Submit to the Canvas assignment VR 3.A: Fire and Light.
