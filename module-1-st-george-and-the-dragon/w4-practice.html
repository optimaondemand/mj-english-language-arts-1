📚 BEFORE YOU START
This is optional extra practice. Nothing here is graded. Use it if you want more reps on this week's grammar and vocabulary.
⚡ GRAMMAR PRACTICE · THINK, THEN REVEAL
Each sentence has a subordinate clause. Decide what relationship it shows first. Then open the box to check.
1. In "Before he was baptized, Marcellus made his confession," what relationship does "before" show? (Decide, then open.)
Time. "Before" places one event ahead of another in time: the confession happens ahead of the baptism.
2. In "Unless he recanted, the emperor threatened to strip his rank," what relationship does "unless" show? (Decide, then open.)
Condition. "Unless" makes the result (keeping or losing his rank) depend on whether he recants.
3. In "Though he feared the cost, Marcellus chose the water," what relationship does "though" show? (Decide, then open.)
Concession. "Though" admits something (his fear) that pushes against the main idea, and he chooses the water anyway.
⚡ VOCABULARY PRACTICE · HOW WELL DOES IT FIT?
Read each sentence. Rate how well the bold word fits the situation it describes.
1. "Marcellus felt genuine sorrow and turned away from his old cruelty: he began to repent."
2. "Marcellus was humble about defeating the dragon, boasting to every soldier he passed."
3. "Pasikrates agreed to be his sponsor, though he barely knew Marcellus and would only sign a paper."
