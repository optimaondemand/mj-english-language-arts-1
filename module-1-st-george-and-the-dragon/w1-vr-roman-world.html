📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Think of VR here as a giant whiteboard you can walk around in. It is a tool for thinking, like a word processor is a tool for writing. Today you will use it to brainstorm: you will gather what Chapters 1–4 show about Marcellus and the world he lives in, and arrange it into a board. That board becomes your plan for the first panel of your Time Capsule storyboard (you build that panel in VR 1.B).
You are not visiting a ready-made world. You are building a board of your own ideas, using the details you found in your reading.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What does Marcellus's world expect of him, and what kind of man is it shaping?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place notes and simple objects. [Teacher: confirm the board/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Reading Journal (the Word doc you set up in the Week-1 Reading Journal lesson), open to your Week 1 notes.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a blank board (or clear space) in your VR app. This is your workspace.
2
Make five or six notes from Chapters 1–4, one detail each, from your reading journal. For example: Marcellus is a tribune who led a raid going first into danger; he fights off bandits on the road; he visits his father Titus's estate; a feast is thrown in his honor; Regina sends him a silver dragon armband. Use the details you actually noticed.
3
Arrange your notes into two groups: "What his world expects of him" and "What kind of man he is." Move them around until the grouping makes sense to you. This is the brainstorming.
4
Add one more note that answers the focus question in a sentence. This note becomes the idea behind your first storyboard panel.
5
Take a screenshot of your finished board. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 1.A: Brainstorm Board. (No headset? Build the same board on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board has five or six real details from Chapters 1–4.
☐
I grouped them into "what his world expects" and "what kind of man he is."
☐
I added a sentence answering the focus question, and submitted a screenshot to VR 1.A.
