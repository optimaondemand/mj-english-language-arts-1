Grammar & Vocabulary · Week 3
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You own a word when you can bend it into your own sentence.
This week's words belong to the dragon's attack and its aftermath: some about destruction and rage, some about grief, planning, and comfort. You'll meet them in context, then make one your own by rewriting a sentence three ways.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use this week's words correctly in the context of the story.
2
I will be able to take one sentence and rewrite it three ways to make a word my own.
⚡ WARM-UP
Which word feels stronger to you: "endure" or "win"? (No wrong answer.)
📚 THIS WEEK'S WORDS, WHERE THEY LIVE

Every word below appears in Chapters 10–14, where the dragon attacks the estate, Marcellus's father is struck down, and a Christian slave is buried. Some carry the heat of the attack; others describe grief, planning, and quiet strength. Notice how the writer moves between words of rage and words of comfort.

⚡ TAP TO REVEAL · A WORD FAMILY
intercede → what's the noun form? (tap)
The noun is intercession. When Regina intercedes with the dragon for Marcellus, she makes an intercession. Many words have a family: mourn/mourning, strategy/strategic, reckless/recklessness. Learning one form helps you spot the rest.
✏️ THE WRITTEN PRACTICE · MAKE A WORD YOUR OWN
Pick one word from the table. Find (or write) a sentence that uses it. Then rewrite that sentence three ways in your Reading Journal:
  1. Swap: replace the word with a near-synonym. Does the meaning shift?
  2. Stretch: keep the word but add a prepositional phrase or two to put a reader in the scene.
  3. Imitate: keep the sentence's shape but write about something in your own life.
Finish with one line: which version is strongest, and why?
📖 FROM THE BOOK
Hunt for two of this week's words inside Chapters 10–14. (Read from your own copy of the book.) Copy each full sentence into your journal; one becomes your rewrite sentence.
⚡ TRY IT · THE RIGHT WORD
Choose the word that fits each sentence from the world of the novel.
1. When his father was struck down, Marcellus was filled with a desire for ______ against the dragon.
2. Regina promises to ______ with the dragon for Marcellus, begging it to accept his sacrifice.
3. Weighed down by grief and the dragon's influence, Marcellus felt ______ and could barely act.
