📖 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
St. George and the Dragon
Michael Lotti, Chapters 1–4
This is your first entry in the Time Capsule Storyboard, a six-panel sequence you build across the whole module, one panel each week. By Week 6 the storyboard tells the whole story of Marcellus's journey from Roman soldier to Christian martyr. This week you build Panel 1: The Ordinary World, a scene from Chapters 1–4 showing Marcellus's life as a Roman soldier before anything changes. Story stage: the ordinary world, before the story's call arrives.
🏭 COMPOSITIONAL CRAFT
Rendering a text in another medium: when you arrange a scene to depict Marcellus's ordinary world, you are making an interpretive claim about what that world looks like. Every choice you make (what you include, what you leave out, how you position things) is a reading of the text. Turning a written scene into a built scene is one of the best ways to understand it: to do it well, you have to understand the original deeply.
Virtue and vice in space: the Ordinary World the novel opens in is built on demanded conformity, a kind of tyranny that looks like order. Ask yourself what that looks like when you arrange it in three dimensions. Where is the power? Where is the person who has to serve it?
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What does the Ordinary World ask of Marcellus, and what does your panel show about who he is before the story truly begins?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset
Activity type: solo; you build your storyboard on your own, at your own pace
Tools you'll use: an open space to build in, simple objects to arrange, and a writable surface or note tool for your labels. [Teacher: confirm the build/objects/notes tools students use in the current VR platform]
To start: open a blank space. You are not touring a ready-made world; you assemble this scene yourself from objects you choose.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a blank space in your VR app to build in. This is your workspace.
2
Choose one specific scene from Chapters 1–4 to depict: Marcellus reporting to Demetrios in the tent, fighting the bandits on the road, sharing the quiet meal with his father, or unwrapping Regina's dragon armband. Pick one clear moment, not a summary of all four chapters.
3
Arrange 3–5 objects from your VR app's library to build your storyboard panel. Your composition should show the setting, a sense of Marcellus's world, and the feel of the scene you chose.
4
Add two labels using a writable surface or note tool (or, if your app has none, jot them in your M365 Reading Journal and keep them in frame on a card): (a) a title naming the scene you depicted, e.g. "Marcellus Receives His Orders"; (b) a stage label reading "Story Stage: The Ordinary World," plus one sentence on what this world demands of Marcellus in your scene. Sign each with your name.
5
Take a screenshot of your finished panel with both labels visible.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My panel shows 3–5 objects depicting a specific scene from Chapters 1–4 (not a general summary).
☐
I added two signed labels: a scene title and a story-stage label ("The Ordinary World") with one explanatory sentence.
☐
I have a screenshot showing both labels.
