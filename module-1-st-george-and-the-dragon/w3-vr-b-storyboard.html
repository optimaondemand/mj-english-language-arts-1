📖 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
St. George and the Dragon
Michael Lotti, Chapters 10–14
You build Panel 3: The Road of Trials. The Road of Trials is the stretch where challenges accumulate, when each test forces the hero to rely on something more than old skills and certainties. For Marcellus the trials are both physical (the dragon attacks) and internal (watching the Christians endure). Choose one moment from Chapters 10–14 that best captures a trial, physical or internal, and build it. Story stage: the road of trials, where the tests pile up.
🏭 COMPOSITIONAL CRAFT
Which trial, and why: the trial you choose is an interpretive claim about what kind of difficulty most refines the hero in these chapters. Lotti gives you physical trials (the dragon attacks, the siege, the fire) and internal ones (watching the Christians endure, the first crack in Marcellus's certainty). Choosing which one to build is the same decision Lotti made about which scene to render most fully.
Perseverance rendered spatially: a future viewer should sense the hero is being changed by what he endures, that it is costing him something and giving him something at once. Build so the internal dimension is visible, not just the external action.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What trial from Chapters 10–14 forces Marcellus to draw on something beyond his Roman training, and how does your panel show this is more than a physical test?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset
Activity type: solo; you build your storyboard on your own, at your own pace
Tools you'll use: an open space to build in, simple objects to arrange, and a writable surface or note tool for your labels. [Teacher: confirm the build/objects/notes tools students use in the current VR platform]
To start: open a blank space. You are not touring a ready-made world; you assemble this scene yourself from objects you choose. Place Panel 3 beside your earlier panels if your app keeps them; otherwise build it on its own and keep your earlier screenshots for reference.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a blank space in your VR app to build in. This is your workspace.
2
Choose your trial scene. The Road of Trials should feel harder, more pressured than the earlier panels. Consider the dragon's attack on a village, Christians enduring threat without resistance, or Marcellus watching and not knowing what to do.
3
Arrange 3–5 objects from your VR app's library to build Panel 3.
4
Add two signed labels using a writable surface or note tool (or jot them in your M365 Reading Journal and keep a card in frame): (a) a title naming your scene; (b) a stage label reading "Story Stage: The Road of Trials," plus one sentence on what Marcellus is being tested on internally, not just physically.
5
Take a screenshot with both labels visible. Submit it to the Canvas assignment VR 3.B: Storyboard Week 3.
