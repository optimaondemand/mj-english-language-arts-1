📖 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
St. George and the Dragon
Michael Lotti, Chapters 15–19
You build Panel 4: The Transformation, the turning point of the whole story, the moment the old self ends and the new self begins. For Marcellus it is the baptism and renaming. Choose one moment from Chapters 15–19 (the baptism itself, the first use of the new name, or the first act performed as George) and build it. Story stage: the transformation, where the character crosses into a new identity.
🏭 COMPOSITIONAL CRAFT
The hardest panel to capture: baptism is at once death and rebirth, two things happening in a single moment. The strongest images can hold a contradiction in one frame. Do not show only the joy of transformation or only the cost; show the crossing.
Going down and coming up: going down and coming back changed is one of the most common patterns in old stories the world over. Your panel should show the hero at the moment between the two: no longer what he was, not yet fully what he is becoming. After the pressure of Panel 3, Panel 4 should feel like a release into something new.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What did Marcellus have to give up, and what did he receive, in order to cross this threshold?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset
Activity type: solo; you build your storyboard on your own, at your own pace
Tools you'll use: an open space to build in, simple objects to arrange, and a writable surface or note tool for your labels. [Teacher: confirm the build/objects/notes tools students use in the current VR platform]
To start: open a blank space. You are not touring a ready-made world; you assemble this scene yourself from objects you choose. If your app keeps your earlier panels, place Panel 4 beside them; if not, keep your earlier screenshots for reference.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a blank space in your VR app to build in. This is your workspace. If you can, review your first three panels. Panel 4 should feel like a shift, not just more of the same.
2
Arrange 3–5 objects for your chosen moment of transformation. Think about what can suggest water, light, a threshold crossed, or a new beginning. Grouping and spacing shape meaning: does Panel 4 look different from Panels 2 and 3?
3
Add two signed labels using a writable surface or note tool (or jot them in your M365 Reading Journal and keep a card in frame): (a) a title naming the scene from the novel and the chapter; (b) a stage label reading "Story Stage: The Transformation," plus one sentence answering the focus question above.
4
Take a screenshot of Panel 4 with both labels clearly visible. Submit it to the Canvas assignment VR 4.B: Storyboard Week 4.

☐
