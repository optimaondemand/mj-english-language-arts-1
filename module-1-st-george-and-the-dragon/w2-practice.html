📚 BEFORE YOU START
This is optional extra practice. Nothing here is graded. Use it if you want more reps on this week's grammar and vocabulary.
⚡ GRAMMAR PRACTICE · DOES THE TENSE FIT?
Read each sentence. Decide how well the verb tense fits what the writer is trying to say.
1. "The dragon has guarded that hill for as long as anyone in the village can remember."
2. "Yesterday, the dragon has attacked the flock."
3. "The villagers feared the dragon for generations."
⚡ VOCABULARY PRACTICE · THINK, THEN REVEAL
Answer each in your head first. Then open the box to check.
1. Which word names a person who dies rather than deny what they believe? (Decide, then open.)
martyr. It comes from a Greek word meaning "witness." Agathon says he would rather become one than deny his faith.
2. Which word names an offering, often an animal, given to a powerful being? (Decide, then open.)
sacrifice. The dragon demands sheep or goats as a sacrifice before it will let travelers pass.
3. Which word describes the one soft, scaleless spot on the dragon that can be harmed? (Decide, then open.)
vulnerable. It means open to being harmed or attacked. Marcellus finds the dragon's one vulnerable spot under its wing.
