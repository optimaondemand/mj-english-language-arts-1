📝 WHAT TODAY IS FOR
Six weeks come together today. You write the essay that proves what you think the novel means, record a short memorial spoken in a character's voice, and assemble all your pieces into the finished Time Capsule. Work through the three stops below in order. Each one tells you exactly where to go in Canvas.
⚡ CHECK IN
You're writing the essay that pulls the whole module together. How's your claim feeling? (No wrong answer. This just helps you pick where to slow down.)
📝 STOP 1 · CULMINATING ESSAY (graded)
This week there is no separate weekly write. Your culminating essay is part of the Module Project: St. George Time Capsule. Choose one prompt, make a clear claim about what the novel means, and prove it with evidence from across all six weeks. Use complex sentences and six vocabulary words. This essay is the written reflection inside your Time Capsule. Write it in Microsoft Word and submit it with the rest of your Time Capsule via the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab (see Stop 3). Full directions and the M365 steps are on the Module Project assignment.
➡️ Go to the Module Project · St. George Time Capsule · [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
🧐 STOP 2 · VR ACTIVITY (completion)
Use VR this week to brainstorm and then build your final storyboard panel and record a short memorial spoken in a character's voice (see the VR pages for steps). Imagine you are someone who knew George, speaking about what his life and death meant. Stay true to who that character is and what the novel shows. This recording goes into your Time Capsule.
🧐 Optima's VR platform is being upgraded. Your facilitator will confirm how to open the activity and make the recording in the current app.
➡️ Open this week's VR activity
📚 STOP 3 · MODULE PROJECT · ASSEMBLE & SUBMIT
This is the week your St. George Time Capsule is due. Assemble your six-panel storyboard, your recorded memorial, your written letter, and your culminating essay into one Word document, following the directions and rubric on the Module Project assignment. Then submit it via the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab.
📖 About your Reading Journal: this is the one Word doc you set up in the Week-1 Reading Journal lesson, kept in your OneDrive ELA6 folder. It is one ongoing document, not a separate turn-in. Pull from its "Week-by-Week Notes" section as you assemble the Time Capsule. Your teacher checks the journal for completion at the end of this week (the final biweekly Reading Journal Check), so make sure your Week 5 and Week 6 notes are in and complete.
➡️ Go to the Module Project · St. George Time Capsule · [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
💪 YOU'RE DONE WHEN
You've submitted your culminating essay, recorded your memorial, and turned in your finished Time Capsule. That's the whole module, from the Roman world to a legacy that outlasts it. Well done.
