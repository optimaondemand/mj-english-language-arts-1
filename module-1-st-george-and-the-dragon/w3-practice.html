📚 BEFORE YOU START
This is optional extra practice. Nothing here is graded. Use it if you want more reps on this week's grammar and vocabulary.
⚡ GRAMMAR PRACTICE · TAP TO REVEAL WHAT IT ANSWERS
Read the bold phrase. Decide what question it answers: where, when, how, or why / despite what. Then click the card to check.
The Christians gathered beneath the old olive tree. (tap)
Where. "Beneath the old olive tree" places the reader in a location.
The guards searched the estate after midnight. (tap)
When. "After midnight" tells you the time of the search.
Marcellus spoke despite his fear. (tap)
Why / despite what. "Despite his fear" admits something working against the action, and Marcellus speaks anyway.
⚡ VOCABULARY PRACTICE · THE RIGHT WORD
Choose the word that fits each sentence from the world of the novel. Pick one for instant feedback.
1. The soldiers planned their next move with care; every step was part of a careful ______.
2. After the burial, the household gathered to ______ together.
3. Her kind words gave him some ______ in his grief.
