You've read, you've studied words and craft, and you've checked your understanding. 📚 STOP 3 · MODULE PROJECT · ONE STEP
Your end-of-module project is the St. George Time Capsule. This week's brick: in your Module Organizer (the Word doc in your OneDrive ELA6 folder), jot which character's voice you might speak in, and paste in the one detail you found about Marcellus's world. Small steps now make the final week easy.
📖 About your Reading Journal: this is the one Word doc you set up in the Week-1 Reading Journal lesson, kept in your OneDrive ELA6 folder. It is one ongoing document, not a separate weekly turn-in. Keep adding to its "Week-by-Week Notes" section as you read. Your teacher checks the journal for completion every two weeks (the first check is at the end of Week 2), so keep it up to date.
