⚡ VOCABULARY PRACTICE · TAP TO REVEAL
Read the word. Try to recall what it means from Chapters 1–4. Then click the card to check.
tribune (tap to see the meaning)
An officer of high rank in the Roman army who commanded a body of soldiers. This is Marcellus's rank.
bandit (tap to see the meaning)
A robber who roams and attacks travelers, often as part of a group. A camp of these ambushes Marcellus on the road.
discipline (tap to see the meaning)
Strict training that enforces order and obedience; self-control. Marcellus's whole upbringing runs on it.
estate (tap to see the meaning)
A large area of country land with a house, farms, and workers, owned by one family. Titus's household lives on one.
hospitality (tap to see the meaning)
The generous, friendly welcome and care a host gives guests. It is a serious duty in the Roman world of this novel.
