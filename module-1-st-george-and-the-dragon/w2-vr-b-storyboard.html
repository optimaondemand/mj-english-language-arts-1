📖 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
St. George and the Dragon
Michael Lotti, Chapters 5–9
This is your second storyboard entry: Panel 2: The Call to Adventure. This is the stage where the ordinary world is disrupted, when something arrives that cannot be ignored and pulls the main character toward a new path. For Marcellus, it is the encounter with both the dragon and the Christians. Choose one scene from Chapters 5–9 that best captures this disruption and build it. Your panel should show not just what happens, but what changes for Marcellus. Story stage: the call that disrupts the ordinary world.
🏭 COMPOSITIONAL CRAFT
Your panel is your reading: the scene you choose and how you depict it is an interpretive claim about what the call looks like for Marcellus. This is the moment a darker, threatening world makes its claim felt, when the ordinary world can no longer go on without noticing what threatens it. Your composition is your argument about which moment that is.
Building on Panel 1: if you can, place Panel 2 so a viewer moving from Panel 1 to Panel 2 can feel that something has changed. The arrangement should embody the disruption before the labels explain it.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What is the moment (the encounter, the sight, the question) that Marcellus cannot ignore or unfeel? How does your panel show this is a turning point?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset
Activity type: solo; you build your storyboard on your own, at your own pace
Tools you'll use: an open space to build in, simple objects to arrange, and a writable surface or note tool for your labels. [Teacher: confirm the build/objects/notes tools students use in the current VR platform]
To start: open a blank space. You are not touring a ready-made world; you assemble this scene yourself from objects you choose. If your app keeps your earlier work, place Panel 2 beside Panel 1; if not, keep a screenshot of Panel 1 for reference.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a blank space in your VR app to build in. This is your workspace.
2
Choose your scene from Chapters 5–9: a specific encounter, not a summary. Good options: Marcellus first seeing the dragon worshipped, his first encounter with the Christians, or the moment something unsettles his Roman certainties.
3
Arrange 3–5 objects from your VR app's library to build Panel 2. Think about how it relates to Panel 1: can a viewer feel that something has changed?
4
Add two signed labels using a writable surface or note tool (or jot them in your M365 Reading Journal and keep a card in frame): (a) a title naming your scene, e.g. "Marcellus Watches the Christians Pray"; (b) a stage label reading "Story Stage: The Call to Adventure," plus one sentence on what this call asks of Marcellus that his ordinary world never did.
5
Take a screenshot of Panel 2 (ideally with Panel 1 in the background) with both labels visible. Submit it to the Canvas assignment VR 2.B: Storyboard Week 2.
