⚡ GRAMMAR PRACTICE · PHRASES AND THE NOUNS THEY DESCRIBE
Answer each question about the participial phrase. Pick one for instant feedback.
1. "Gripping the reins tightly, George steadied his horse." Whom does the phrase describe?
2. Which sentence has a dangling (misplaced) participle?
3. In "Exhausted but unbroken, George rose to fight again," which word is a past participle?
⚡ VOCABULARY PRACTICE · TAP TO REVEAL
Read the word. Try to recall what it means from Chapters 20–21. Then click the card to check.
courage (tap to see the meaning)
The ability to face danger or pain without being overcome by fear. George needs it to go out and face the dragon.
provisions (tap to see the meaning)
Supplies of food and gear gathered for a journey. The men pack these before setting out to face the dragon.
lunge (tap to see the meaning)
A sudden forward thrust or spring. George uses one to strike at the dragon in the fight.
bellow (tap to see the meaning)
To make a deep, loud roaring sound. The wounded dragon lets out one when it is struck.
grim (tap to see the meaning)
Stern, serious, and unsmiling, especially about something hard. The men's faces turn this way before the fight.
