⚡ MORE PRACTICE · NOUNS AND PRONOUNS
Answer each row about nouns and pronouns. Pick one and you'll get instant feedback.
1. Which word is the noun? "The tired travelers reached the castle."
2. Which sentence has the clearest pronoun? "Merlin warned Uther that he would not live long."
3. "The young knight raised his sword, and ______ charged." Which pronoun matches?
⚡ MORE PRACTICE · WORDS OF KINGSHIP
Tap each card to check the word's meaning from "The Two Swords."
sovereign
(tap to reveal)
A king or supreme ruler; having the highest power. Arthur becomes Britain's sovereign.
destiny
(tap to reveal)
The future that seems set for someone; what a person is meant to become. Arthur's destiny is to be king.
valor
(tap to reveal)
Great courage and bravery, especially in danger or battle.
chivalry
(tap to reveal)
The medieval code of honor, courage, and courtesy that knights were expected to live by.
realm
(tap to reveal)
A kingdom; the land and people a ruler governs, like the Realm of Logres.
anvil
(tap to reveal)
A heavy iron block a blacksmith hammers metal against. The sword in the stone is fixed in one.
