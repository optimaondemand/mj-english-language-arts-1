Put the Week to Work
This is your studio day. No new reading. You write, you build in VR, and you take the next step on the module essay.
📝 WHAT TODAY IS FOR
You've met Arthur and Gawain and studied suspense, conflict, setting, and strong verbs. Today you turn your knight chart into a plan: a working claim about what makes a good knight, and an outline for the essay. Work through the three stops below in order. Each one tells you exactly where to go in Canvas.
⚡ CHECK IN
How clear is your idea of what makes a good knight so far? (No wrong answer. This just helps you pick where to slow down.)
📝 STOP 1 · QUICK WRITE 2 (graded)
This week's write is Quick Write 2: Working Claim and Essay Outline. You write one clear claim answering "what makes a good knight?" and sketch an outline (introduction, three supporting points, conclusion) for your comparison essay. Write it in Microsoft Word and submit via the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab. Full directions and the M365 reminder are on the Canvas assignment.
➡️ Go to Quick Write 2 · [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
🧐 STOP 2 · VR ACTIVITY (completion)
Use VR this week as a thinking-and-building tool: brainstorm Gawain's strengths and his one flaw, then build toward your knight-comparison essay. The VR pages have the full steps.
🧐 Note: Optima's VR platform is being upgraded. Your facilitator will confirm how to open this activity in the current app. The thinking and the thing you build are what matter, and they transfer to any tool.
➡️ Open this week's VR activity · [Teacher: link the VR page]
📚 STOP 3 · MODULE PROJECT · ONE STEP
Your end-of-module project is the "What Makes a Good Knight?" comparison essay. This week's brick: in your Reading Journal (the Word doc in your OneDrive ELA6 folder), add Gawain to your knight chart with one strength and one flaw, each tied to a moment from the chapter. Decide which two knights you might compare in the essay.
📖 Reading Journal Check (this week): your Reading Journal is checked for completion every two weeks, and the first check is at the end of Week 2. Make sure your "Week-by-Week Notes" section is up to date for Weeks 1 and 2, then submit your Reading Journal Word doc through the Canvas OneDrive tab. This is completion-scored, so keeping it current is all you need.
➡️ Go to the Reading Journal Check · [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
💪 YOU'RE DONE WHEN
You've submitted Quick Write 2, recorded your VR activity, updated your knight chart, and turned in your Reading Journal for the Week-2 check. That's a full week. Nicely done.
