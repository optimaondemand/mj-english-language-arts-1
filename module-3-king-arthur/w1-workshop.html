This is your studio day. No new reading. You write, you build in VR, and you take the first step on the module essay.
📝 WHAT TODAY IS FOR
You've read about the two swords, studied words and craft, and checked your understanding. Today you turn that into something of your own: a short write, a scene you build in VR, and the first brick of your module essay. Work through the three stops below in order. Each one tells you exactly where to go in Canvas.
⚡ CHECK IN
How is your thinking feeling after Week 1? (No wrong answer. This just helps you pick where to slow down.)
📝 STOP 1 · QUICK WRITE 1 (graded)
This week's write is Quick Write 1: Knight Comparison Chart. You start a chart of the knights you will compare and write a few sentences on what you think makes a good knight, using Arthur as your first example. Write it in Microsoft Word and submit via the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab. Full directions and the M365 steps are on the Canvas assignment. This is the module's first graded write, so the assignment carries the full M365 Skill Step box.
➡️ Go to Quick Write 1 · [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
🧐 STOP 2 · VR ACTIVITY (completion)
Use VR this week as a thinking-and-building tool: brainstorm what makes a good knight, then start building toward your knight-comparison essay. The VR pages have the full steps.
🧐 Note: Optima's VR platform is being upgraded. Your facilitator will confirm how to open this activity in the current app. The thinking and the thing you build are what matter, and they transfer to any tool.
➡️ Open this week's VR activity · [Teacher: link the VR page]
📚 STOP 3 · MODULE PROJECT · ONE STEP
Your end-of-module project is the "What Makes a Good Knight?" comparison essay. This week's brick: in your Reading Journal (the Word doc in your OneDrive ELA6 folder), start your knight chart with Arthur. Note one trait and one moment from "The Two Swords" that shows it. Small steps now make the final week easy.
➡️ Open your Reading Journal · [Teacher: link the journal setup page]
💪 YOU'RE DONE WHEN
You've submitted Quick Write 1, recorded your VR activity, and started your knight chart in your Reading Journal. That's a full week. Nicely done.
