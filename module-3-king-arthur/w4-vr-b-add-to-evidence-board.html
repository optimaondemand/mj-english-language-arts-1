📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Add Galahad, Percivale, and Launcelot to the Board
Keep growing your one running Knight Evidence board
Since Week 1 you have been building one Knight Evidence board, adding a knight each time you meet one. Now you add the knights from the Grail Quest. By the end of this week your board holds the four knights your essay can compare. You are not starting over. You are placing the Grail evidence from VR 3.4A next to Gawain and Gareth so you can see all of them at once and start noticing which two you most want to compare.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
With all four knights on one board, which trait does each one stand for, and where do they agree or clash?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: the same board you have been growing, or one where you can place and group notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Knight Evidence board so far (Gawain from Week 2, Gareth from Week 3) and your Grail Knights Board screenshot from VR 3.4A.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your Knight Evidence board, the one you started in Week 2. You should already have a card for Gawain (courtesy, his one honest failing) and one for Gareth (humility and service).
2
Add a card for Galahad/Percivale (purity and devotion on the Grail Quest) and a card for Launcelot (greatest in arms, undone by a divided heart). Pull the moments straight from your VR 3.4A board.
3
On each card, make sure you have three things: the knight's name, the trait the text gives him, and one specific moment that proves it. Add a second moment if you have one from your reading.
4
Step back and group the four cards. Place knights with similar traits near each other and pull apart the ones that clash. Add a short note where two knights clash, for example "Launcelot has the most skill but the Grail still passes him by."
5
Take a screenshot of the full board with all four knights. Submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 3.4B: Add to Your Evidence Board. You'll finish this board next week and pick the two knights to compare. (No headset? Build the same four-knight board on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board now has cards for all four knights: Gawain, Gareth, Galahad/Percivale, and Launcelot.
☐
Each card names the knight, his trait, and at least one specific moment from the book.
☐
I grouped the cards and added one note where two knights clash, then submitted a screenshot to VR 3.4B.
