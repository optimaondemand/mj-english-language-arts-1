📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Sir Gawain and the Green Knight
Roger Lancelyn Green · building toward your essay
In VR 3.2A you gathered evidence on Gawain. Now you add him to the Knight Evidence board you started in Week 1. A board is a tool for organizing a comparison, the same way a chart is. Putting Gawain in the same columns as Arthur lets you start seeing how two knights line up, which is exactly the move your essay "What Makes a Good Knight?" asks you to make.
Keep building one board. It grows by one figure each week so the comparison is ready before you draft.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Set side by side, how is Gawain's kind of honor like and unlike the standard "The Two Swords" set up?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can extend labeled columns of notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Knight Evidence board (from Week 1 and VR 3.2A) and your own copy of the book.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your Knight Evidence board. Find the row you left labeled for Gawain.
2
Fill Gawain's row with at least two trait-and-evidence pairs from your brainstorm: in the Trait column, name a quality (courage, courtesy, honesty); in the Evidence column, place the moment that proves it (taking the Green Knight's challenge; refusing the lady courteously; hiding the green lace).
3
Add one note in Gawain's row that names his honest failing and what it costs him (the concealed lace, the nick on the neck). A good comparison keeps the flaw on the board, not just the praise.
4
Draw or place one connector note between Arthur's row and Gawain's that says, in a sentence, how their kinds of worth compare. This is the first comparing sentence your essay can use.
5
Take a screenshot of your board with Arthur and Gawain on it. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 3.2B: Add to the Board. (No headset? Build the same chart on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Gawain's row has at least two trait-and-evidence pairs from the chapter, plus his honest failing.
☐
I added one connector note comparing Gawain's worth with Arthur's.
☐
I submitted a screenshot of my growing board to VR 3.2B.
