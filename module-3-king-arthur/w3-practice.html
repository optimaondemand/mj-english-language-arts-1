⚡ MORE PRACTICE · ADJECTIVE OR ADVERB?
Tap each card to check your answer.
In "the scornful damsel rode ahead," is scornful an adjective or an adverb?
(tap to check)
Adjective. It describes the damsel, a noun, telling us what kind of damsel she is.
In "Beaumains answered gently," is gently an adjective or an adverb?
(tap to check)
Adverb. It describes how he answered, so it modifies the verb "answered."
Which is more precise, "fought well" or "fought fiercely"?
(tap to check)
"Fought fiercely" is more precise. It paints a sharper picture of the action than the vague word "well."
