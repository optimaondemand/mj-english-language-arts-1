📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Sort the Grail Knights: Who Passes, Who Fails
A brainstorm board for the Holy Grail Quest
Your module project asks a hard question: what makes a good knight? The Grail Quest is the book's clearest test of that, because it ranks the knights by something other than strength. This week you use a board to sort the Grail knights into who comes near the Grail and who falls short, and to name what the Grail is actually testing. A board lets you lay them side by side and see the pattern before you write. The evidence you gather here drops straight into the Knight Evidence board you have been building since Week 1.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What is the Grail Quest really testing, and why does the greatest knight in arms not pass it?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place and move notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Reading Journal notes on Book Three (the Grail Quest) and your Knight Evidence board so far.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a board and make two columns: "Comes near the Grail" and "Falls short."
2
Make a note for each Grail knight and place it in a column: Galahad and Percivale on the "comes near" side, Launcelot on the "falls short" side. Under each note, write the one moment from Book Three that puts him there (for example, Galahad takes the Siege Perilous unharmed; Launcelot is allowed only a partial glimpse at Carbonek).
3
Add a note titled "What the Grail tests." In your own words, name the standard it measures. Launcelot is the best knight in arms but does not pass, so the test is not about skill or fame. Write what you think it is testing instead.
4
Draw a line or arrow from "What the Grail tests" to the knight who passes it most fully, and one to the knight who falls short. This is the contrast your essay can use: outward greatness against inner worth.
5
Take a screenshot of your board. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 3.4A: The Grail Knights Board, then carry these notes into VR 3.4B, where you add them to your Knight Evidence board. (No headset? Sort the same knights on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board sorts Galahad, Percivale, and Launcelot into "comes near the Grail" or "falls short," each with one moment from Book Three.
☐
I added a "What the Grail tests" note in my own words and linked it to one knight who passes and one who falls short.
☐
I submitted a screenshot of my board to VR 3.4A.
