📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Who Stays Loyal When Everything Falls Apart?
A brainstorm board on the fall of the Round Table
The fall of the Round Table is the book's hardest test of what makes a knight good, because it tests loyalty when everything is breaking. A good knight is easy to admire in a tournament. The real measure is how he acts when friends turn into enemies and the kingdom comes apart. This week you use a board to gather evidence on the moments of loyalty and betrayal at the end of the book, so you can judge how each knight holds up when the code costs something. This evidence feeds the last cards on your Knight Evidence board.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
When the Round Table breaks, who stays true to the code, and what does that reveal about what makes a knight good?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place and move notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Reading Journal notes on Book Four (the fall and the Last Battle) and your Knight Evidence board so far.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a board and make two zones: "Stays loyal" and "Breaks faith."
2
Drop in notes for the key moments of the fall and sort each one. For example: Mordred seizes the kingdom while Arthur is away; Launcelot rescues Guinevere but kills the unarmed Gareth; Bedivere finally obeys and throws Excalibur into the lake. Under each, write the one knight it best reveals.
3
Add a note titled "Loyalty has a cost." Pick one knight whose loyalty cost him something at the end and write what it cost. This is the kind of evidence that makes an essay argue instead of summarize.
4
Look at your two zones and write one sentence at the top: based on the end of the book, what does Green seem to say a good knight does when the code costs him something?
5
Take a screenshot of your board. Submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 3.5A: Loyalty and Betrayal, then carry these notes into VR 3.5B, where you finish your Knight Evidence board. (No headset? Sort the same moments on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board sorts at least three moments from the end of the book into "stays loyal" or "breaks faith," each tied to a knight.
☐
I added a "Loyalty has a cost" note naming one knight and what his loyalty cost him.
☐
I wrote one sentence on what the end suggests a good knight does, then submitted a screenshot to VR 3.5A.
