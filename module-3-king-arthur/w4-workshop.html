Week 4 · Workshop Day
Put the Week to Work
No new reading today. You strengthen your body paragraphs with evidence, build in VR, join the discussion, and check your journal.
📝 WHAT TODAY IS FOR
The Grail Quest gave you sharp evidence about what makes a knight good: purity over power, worth over fame. Today you turn the week into your own work: revise your essay's body paragraphs, build in VR, join the class discussion, and submit your Reading Journal for its second check. Work through the stops in order.
⚡ CHECK IN
Your draft has body paragraphs. How strong is the evidence in them right now? (No wrong answer. This just helps you pick where to slow down.)
📝 STOP 1 · QUICK WRITE (graded)
This week's Quick Write is a revision of your body paragraphs: mark each paragraph's topic sentence, evidence, and explanation, then add one new specific moment from the book and one sentence explaining it. Work in Microsoft Word and submit via the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab. Full directions and the M365 reminder are on the Canvas assignment.
➡️ Go to the Week 4 Quick Write · [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
🧐 STOP 2 · VR ACTIVITY (completion)
Use VR this week as a thinking-and-building tool. In a virtual space of your choice, brainstorm the symbols of the Grail Quest (the Siege Perilous, the white-samite Grail, the contrast of Galahad and Launcelot), then build toward your knight-comparison essay by laying out evidence for one of your two knights. The VR page has the full steps.
🧐 Optima's VR platform is being upgraded. Your facilitator will confirm how to open the activity in the current app. The thinking and the thing you build are what matter, and they transfer to any tool.
➡️ Open the Week 4 VR activity · [Teacher: link the VR instruction page]
💬 STOP 3 · CLASS DISCUSSION (graded)
Post your claim and reply to a classmate in this week's Canvas Discussion: Is Launcelot a failure for winning only a partial vision of the Grail, or is a partial vision its own kind of honor? Make one clear claim with one piece of evidence, then respond to someone who saw it differently.
➡️ Go to the Week 4 Discussion · [Teacher: link the Canvas discussion]
📚 STOP 4 · MODULE PROJECT & JOURNAL CHECK
In your Module Organizer, paste your revised body paragraphs and confirm each one now has at least one specific moment from the book plus a sentence explaining it. Next week you'll polish the introduction and conclusion.
📖 Reading Journal Check (this week): your Reading Journal is the one Word doc you set up in the Week-1 Reading Journal lesson, kept in your OneDrive ELA6 folder. It is one ongoing document, not a separate weekly turn-in. Your teacher checks it for completion every two weeks, and the second check is now, at the end of Week 4. Make sure your "Week-by-Week Notes" are up to date through Week 4, then submit the journal via the Canvas OneDrive tab.
➡️ Open your Module Organizer · [Teacher: link the organizer page]
💪 YOU'RE DONE WHEN
You've submitted your revised body paragraphs, recorded your VR activity, posted and replied in the discussion, updated your Module Organizer, and turned in your Reading Journal for the Week-4 check. Strong week.
