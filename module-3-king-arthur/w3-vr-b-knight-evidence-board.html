📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Sir Gareth, or The Knight of the Kitchen
Roger Lancelyn Green · building toward your essay
In VR 3.3A you gathered evidence on Gareth. Now you add him to the Knight Evidence board and use the full board to do the most important planning move of the module: choose the two knights you will compare in your essay "What Makes a Good Knight?" A board is a tool for organizing a comparison, the same way an outline is. With Arthur, Gawain, and Gareth side by side, you can see which pairing gives you the sharpest argument.
You may compare any two of Lancelot, Gawain, Gareth, or Percivale in the essay. The board you have built makes that choice with evidence, not a guess.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Looking across your board, which two knights give you the strongest, fairest comparison, and why?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can extend labeled columns of notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Knight Evidence board (Arthur and Gawain rows from Weeks 1–2 and VR 3.3A) and your own copy of the book.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your Knight Evidence board and find the row you left labeled for Gareth.
2
Fill Gareth's row with at least two trait-and-evidence pairs from your brainstorm: in the Trait column, name a quality (humility, patience, prowess); in the Evidence column, place the moment that proves it (asking only for meat and drink; enduring Kay's jeers; winning the scornful damsel's respect on the road).
3
Step back and read your whole board. Mark the two knights you will compare in your essay (any two of Lancelot, Gawain, Gareth, or Percivale). Pick the pairing where the traits clearly differ, so the comparison has something to argue.
4
Add one claim note: in one sentence, say which of your two knights better fits what the book treats as a good knight, and why. This sentence becomes the seed of your essay's claim. Note any evidence you still need to gather in the weeks ahead.
5
Take a screenshot of your board showing your two chosen knights and your claim note. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 3.3B: Add to the Board. (No headset? Build the same chart on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐

☐
I marked the two knights I will compare and wrote a one-sentence claim note.
☐
I submitted a screenshot of my board, with my two knights and claim, to VR 3.3B.
