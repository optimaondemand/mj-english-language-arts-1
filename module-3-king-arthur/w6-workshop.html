🧐 STOP 2 · VR ACTIVITY (completion)
Two short VR moves finish your argument. First, plan your essay on a board (VR 6.A): lay out your claim and your evidence for each knight in space so the shape of the essay is clear. Then rehearse your argument aloud (VR 6.B): say, in a sentence or two for each knight, what makes him a good knight and where he falls short. This is your essay, spoken aloud. The VR pages have the full steps.
