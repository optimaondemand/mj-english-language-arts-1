⚡ MORE PRACTICE · SENTENCE TYPES AND CONJUNCTIONS
Write one compound sentence about a knight of your choice, using the conjunction "but."
Reveal a model sentence
One strong option: "Percivale searched for years, but he almost gave up hope." A compound sentence joins two complete ideas with a comma and a coordinating conjunction like "but." Compare: does your sentence have two complete ideas joined by "but"?
Write one complex sentence about a knight, starting with the subordinating conjunction "because."
Reveal a model sentence
One strong option: "Because Galahad's heart was pure, he could sit in the Siege Perilous." The part starting with "because" cannot stand alone; it depends on the rest of the sentence. Compare: does the "because" part of your sentence depend on the rest?
Combine these two choppy sentences into one: "Bors was tempted. Bors resisted." Write your combined sentence.
Reveal one strong way to say it
One strong option: "Bors was tempted, but he resisted." The conjunction "but" shows the tension between the two ideas instead of just listing them side by side.
⚡ MORE PRACTICE · WORDS OF THE GRAIL QUEST
Choose the word that fits each sentence from the world of the quest.
1. A ______ living alone in the forest guided Galahad toward the Siege Perilous.
2. Only a knight of complete ______ of heart could sit in that seat and live.
3. In the end, only one knight proved ______ enough to achieve the Grail.
