📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
King Arthur and His Knights of the Round Table
Roger Lancelyn Green · building toward your essay
In VR 3.1A you gathered evidence. Now you organize it into a comparison board you will add to every week. A board is a tool for organizing an argument, the same way a chart is. You will set up columns so that, by Week 3, you can line up several knights side by side, compare their traits, and pick the two you will write about in your essay "What Makes a Good Knight?"
Keep this board. It grows by one figure each week and becomes the backbone of your essay.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What traits does "The Two Swords" set up as the standard a good knight is measured against?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can set up labeled columns of notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your brainstorm board from VR 3.1A and your own copy of the book.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your Knight Evidence board. Set up three column headers along the top: Knight, Trait it tests, and Evidence from the book. You will keep adding rows under these headers as the module goes on.
2
Make your first row for Arthur. In the Trait column, name one quality "The Two Swords" highlights (for example, the humility to draw the sword without claiming credit, or keeping a sworn promise to the Lady of the Lake). In the Evidence column, place the moment from the book that shows it.
3
Add a second row for Arthur with a different trait and a different moment, so each figure on your board carries at least two trait-and-evidence pairs. This is the level of detail your essay paragraphs will need.
4
Leave empty rows below, labeled for the knights still to come: Gawain, Gareth, Lancelot, and Percivale. Seeing the gaps now tells you what evidence to hunt for in the coming weeks.
5
Take a screenshot of your board so far. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 3.1B: Build the Board. (No headset? Build the same chart on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board has Knight, Trait, and Evidence columns.
☐
Arthur has at least two trait-and-evidence rows, with real moments from "The Two Swords."
☐
I left labeled rows for the knights still to come and submitted a screenshot to VR 3.1B.
