⚡ MORE PRACTICE · SENTENCE VARIETY
For each item, pick the answer along the row. You'll get instant feedback.
1. Combine: "Percivale searched. He never found the Grail." Which combined version is strongest?
2. Reshape the opening: "Percivale searched for many years." Which reshaped version works best?
3. After three long sentences about the quest, you write: "The Grail was gone." This short sentence is…
⚡ MORE PRACTICE · WORDS FOR THE ESSAY
Choose the word that fits each sentence about the knights.
1. Percivale's single-minded search for the Grail shows his ______ to the quest.
2. Even though he seemed the greatest of all, Launcelot's divided heart proved to be his ______.
3. Setting Gareth's humility beside Kay's scorn draws a sharp ______ between the two.
