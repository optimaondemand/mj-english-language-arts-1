📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Choose Two, Lay Them Side by Side
The finished Knight Evidence board for your essay
Your board now holds all four knights with evidence from across the book. This week you make the choice your essay needs: pick the two knights you will compare, and lay them side by side so the comparison is built before you write. A good pairing gives you something to argue, not just two lists. When the two knights are face to face on the board with their best evidence lined up, the essay almost plans itself.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Which two knights, set side by side, give you the sharpest argument about what makes a good knight?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: the same board you have been growing, where you can move and line up notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your full Knight Evidence board (all four knights) and your VR 3.5A loyalty notes.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your full Knight Evidence board. Add any last moments from this week's reading (the fall and the Last Battle) to the cards that need them, so every card is complete.
2
Pick the two knights you will compare in your essay. Choose a pair that clashes in an interesting way, for example Launcelot (greatest in arms, divided heart) against Galahad or Percivale (pure, achieves the Grail), or Gawain (courtesy with one honest failing) against Gareth (humble service).
3
Move those two cards into the center and lay them side by side. Set the rest of the board aside. For each of your two knights, line up an early, a middle, and a late moment from the book, so your evidence stretches across the whole story.
4
Between the two knights, add one note: in one sentence, which knight better fits what you think makes a good knight, and why. That sentence is the start of your essay's claim.
5
Take a screenshot of your two knights side by side with their lined-up evidence. Submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 3.5B: Pick Your Two Knights. Next week you turn this into your essay plan. (No headset? Lay the same two knights side by side on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board is finished, and I chose the two knights I will compare.
☐
The two knights sit side by side, each with an early, a middle, and a late moment from the book.
☐
I added one sentence on which knight better fits a good knight and why, then submitted a screenshot to VR 3.5B.
