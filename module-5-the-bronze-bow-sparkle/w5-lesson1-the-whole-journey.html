The one sentence your whole essay sets out to prove. It says what you think and why, and a careful reader could argue against it.

Imani’s introduction
6th Grade Scholar

At eight years old, Daniel bar Jamin makes a vow to pay the Romans back with his whole life (Ch. 7). For years that vow is all he lives for. Three voices try to win him: Rosh, who says to take what the fight needs by force; Hezron, who says to wait and keep the Law; and the teacher from Nazareth, who says that hate itself is the enemy. In The Bronze Bow, Elizabeth George Speare shows that hatred traps the person who carries it, and only love sets Daniel free.
