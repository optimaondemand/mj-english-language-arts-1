The list that changes shape

The colon with no sentence before it
Daniel faced: Rosh and Hezron.→Daniel faced two voices: Rosh and Hezron.
The phrase beside the wrong person
Swept up by Joel’s passion, the oath was sworn.→Swept up by Joel’s passion, Daniel swore the oath.

Five lenses for your whole essay
  1. Claim. The last sentence of your introduction is marked. Does it say what and why?
  2. Evidence by chapter. Every chapter you name is marked. Three different chapters are needed.
  3. Other side. The opener of the other side and the turn word of the answer are found.
  4. Craft items. Your marks are counted. Colons and semicolons are checked.
  5. Five module words. Words from the module’s five lists are found.

    ✅ Final Self-Check
    The four things the Module Project is read for. Not scored.
    Understanding 30 points on the rubric
    Every moment I use really happens, at the chapter I name.
    My essay shows the difference between Rosh’s way and the teacher’s.
    Reasoning 30 points on the rubric
    My thesis says what and why, and someone could argue back.
    I state the other side the way someone who believes it would.
    My answer uses a different moment and really answers it.
    I use three moments, each named by chapter, each with why.
    Clarity and precision 25 points on the rubric
    Five module words are underlined.
    One periodic sentence (PS) and one participial phrase (PP) are underlined and marked.
    I used parallel structure where Rosh’s view meets the teacher’s.
    My colons and semicolons have full sentences where they need them.
    The Works Cited line is at the end.
    The title is in Heading 1 and the body font is set.
    Honesty 15 points on the rubric
    The essay is in my own words.
    I used no page numbers, only chapters.
    I did not make the other side weak on purpose.
