How it is scored: 25 points
1. Set-up and context5 pts
Full creditChapter named, with the first and last words of the passage. Two sentences say accurately what happens in the passage.
PartialLocation named, but the two sentences are vague or describe the wrong thing.
Not yetNo set-up.
2. Accuracy of reading5 pts
Full creditReads the passage as written. Unfamiliar words and names are attempted and pronounced clearly.
PartialFrequent misreadings, or lines skipped.
Not yetPassage not read, or far under 300 words.
3. Pace, volume, and expression8 pts
Full creditSteady pace a listener can follow. Audible throughout. Phrasing and emphasis match what the sentence means.
PartialRushed, flat, or hard to hear in places.
Not yetInaudible or unintelligible.
4. The response7 pts
Full creditCompletes the chosen response (Talk it through, Make the case, or Change one thing) and points at something specific in the passage just read.
PartialAnswers one question, or answers generally without pointing at the passage.
Not yetNo reflection.
