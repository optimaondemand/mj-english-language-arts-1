📝 WHAT TODAY IS FOR
You have your reading, your evidence, your outline from Week 6, and now the quoting and citing moves from this week's grammar lesson. Today you write the complete draft of your "Portrait of a Hero" essay and build a first version of your Forest Court in VR. Work through the stops in order. Each one tells you where to go in Canvas.
⚡ CHECK IN
Before you draft: which part of your essay feels least settled right now? (No wrong answer. This helps you focus.)
📝 STOP 1 · QUICK WRITE 7 · FULL ESSAY DRAFT (graded)
This week's Quick Write is the complete first draft of your "Portrait of a Hero" essay: introduction with a thesis, three body paragraphs (topic sentence, evidence, explanation), a concession, and a conclusion. Use this week's reading for evidence and this week's grammar lesson to introduce and cite every quote. Your facilitator leaves comments in your Word doc so you can revise for the Week 8 final. Full directions and the M365 reminder are on the Canvas assignment.
➡️ Go to Quick Write 7 · [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
🧐 STOP 2 · VR ACTIVITY (completion)
Build the first version of your Forest Court (VR 7.B): lay out your argument in space. Place an object for your thesis at the center, then arrange an object for each body-paragraph piece of evidence around it so the shape of your argument is visible. This is your essay, built as a scene you can walk through. The VR page has the full steps.
🧐 Optima's VR platform is being upgraded. Your facilitator will confirm how to open the activity in the current app. The thinking and the thing you build are what matter, and they transfer to any tool.

💪 YOU'RE DONE WHEN
You have submitted a complete essay draft and built a first Forest Court in VR. Next week you revise the draft into your final essay and finish the Forest Court. The hard thinking is done. Now you sharpen it.
