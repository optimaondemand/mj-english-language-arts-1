📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
The Adventures of Robin Hood
Roger Lancelyn Green · building toward your essay
In VR 4.1A you gathered evidence. Now you sort it into a two-column board you will add to every week. A board is a tool for organizing an argument, the same way a chart is. You set up one column for evidence Robin is a hero and one for evidence Robin is an outlaw, so that by the time you write, you can weigh both sides and take a position in your essay "Portrait of a Hero".
Keep this board. It grows with new evidence each week and becomes the backbone of your essay.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
From the opening chapters, which evidence points to "hero" and which points to "outlaw"?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can set up two labeled columns of notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your brainstorm board from VR 4.1A and your own copy of the book.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your Hero or Outlaw? board. Set up two column headers along the top: Evidence he is a HERO and Evidence he is an OUTLAW. You will keep adding notes under these two headers all module long.
2
From your brainstorm, move at least two details into the HERO column (for example, Robin is charged to help the down-trodden, or an unseen archer stops the cruel blinding of Much). For each, add a few words on why it counts as heroic.
3
Move at least one detail into the OUTLAW column (for example, by the end of Chapter 2 Robin is, by law, a declared outlaw with his lands seized). Add a note on whether this label is fair, given how it happened.
4
Leave both columns open with room to grow. Add a small note to yourself naming what you still want to find: does Robin keep acting with a code, or does he become a true criminal? Seeing the gap now tells you what to look for in the coming weeks.
5
Take a screenshot of your board so far. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 4.1B: Build the Board. (No headset? Build the same chart on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board has a HERO column and an OUTLAW column.
☐
I sorted at least three real details from the opening chapters into the two columns and noted why each fits.
☐
I left both columns open to grow and submitted a screenshot to VR 4.1B.
