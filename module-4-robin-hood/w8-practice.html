📚 VOCABULARY · WORDS OF SANCTUARY AND LEGACY
Flip each card to check yourself on this week's words from Chapters 22–24 and the Epilogue.
⚡ CHECK YOURSELF · WORD FLIP
Read each clue and say the word out loud (or in your head) before you flip. Then check yourself.
Safety given to someone in a holy place, such as a church or nunnery. (Say the word, then tap to flip)
sanctuary. Marian seeks sanctuary with the Prioress of Kirkleys.
A secret betrayal of trust. (Say the word, then tap to flip)
treachery. The Prioress's treachery, not an open enemy, ends Robin's life.
What a person leaves behind after death: a reputation, an example, or a story. (Say the word, then tap to flip)
legacy. Robin's legacy is the legend that outlives him.
Kindness or forgiveness shown to someone you could harm or punish instead. (Say the word, then tap to flip)
mercy. Robin shows mercy toward the Prioress even as she betrays him.
Describing a sad ending in which a good or great person comes to ruin or death. (Say the word, then tap to flip)
tragic. The Epilogue frames Robin's death as tragic even as his legend endures.
