📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Grow the Board You Have Been Building
The Hero-versus-Outlaw evidence board for your essay
Since the start of the module you have been growing a Hero-versus-Outlaw evidence board in VR, with a Hero side and an Outlaw side. This week you add the evidence from Chapters 12 to 14, using the method notes you made in VR 4.5A. By the end you should be able to look at the board and see your evidence stacking up on each side. That board is the raw material your "Portrait of a Hero" essay will be built from.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
After this week's reading, which side of your board is getting stronger, and which single piece of evidence is your best?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: the same evidence board you have been growing, with a Hero side and an Outlaw side, where you can place and move notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your evidence board so far (from earlier weeks) and your VR 4.5A method notes.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your evidence board. Confirm it still has two clear sides: a Hero side and an Outlaw side. If you are just starting the board, make those two sides now.
2
Move your three method notes from VR 4.5A onto the board, each on the side it fits: helping the poor and righting Allin-a-Dale's wrong lean Hero; robbing travelers and breaking up a lawful wedding lean Outlaw. Some notes can sit between the two sides if they truly belong to both.
3
Add a chapter and page number to each new note, so your essay can cite it later. A note without a location is hard to use as evidence.
4
Step back and look at the whole board. Mark your single strongest piece of evidence so far with a star or a colored note. That is the piece your essay will likely lead with.
5
Take a screenshot of your updated board. Submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 4.5B: Add to the Evidence Board. Next week you finish the board and decide your position. (No headset? Keep the same two-sided chart on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board has a Hero side and an Outlaw side, with this week's notes placed on the side each one fits.
☐
Each new note has a chapter and page number, and I marked my single strongest piece of evidence.
☐
I submitted a screenshot of my updated evidence board to VR 4.5B.
