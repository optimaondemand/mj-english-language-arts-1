📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A big idea a story keeps returning to is called its theme.
Robin is alone no longer. In Chapter 3 a hundred men gather around him in Sherwood, all clad in Lincoln green. As they form, a big idea begins to take shape: that the outlaws build a fairer, more loyal community in the forest than the one the law offers in the towns. A central idea a story explores is its theme. Today you start tracking it.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to explain what a theme is and name one theme forming in Chapter 3.
2
I will be able to point to one detail that shows loyalty inside the band.

⚡ WARM-UP · CHECK IN FIRST
What holds a group of people together when they have no money, no titles, and no protection from the law? (No wrong answer. Just check in.)
📚 THE LESSON

A theme is a central idea a story keeps exploring. It is not the plot, which is the events, but the meaning underneath them. In Chapter 3, Green gathers "an hundred valiant men" around Robin, all dressed in Lincoln green, and a theme starts to take shape: in the greenwood, the outlaws build the loyalty and fairness that the towns have lost. Look at the proof Green gives you. The men share one color and one leader. Even Marian, whose father renounces Robin to stay safe with those in power, gives her heart to Robin instead. The same Lincoln green that marks a man as an outlaw to the Sheriff marks him as a brother inside the band. A theme is rarely stated in one sentence. You build it from repeated details, so your job today is to collect the details that show what the forest community stands for. Keep asking: what idea is Green returning to again and again?

📖 NOW READ
Read Chapter 3, "The Outlaws of Sherwood Forest." As you read, find one detail that shows loyalty or fairness inside Robin's band. Record it in your Reading Journal.

⚡ CHECK YOURSELF · READING CLOSELY
For each detail from Chapter 3, choose what the writer is mainly building toward. Pick one and you'll get instant feedback.

2. Lord Fitzwalter renounces Robin to stay safe, but Marian's heart stays "wholly his."
3. The Lincoln green that brands a man an outlaw to the Sheriff is a badge of brotherhood in Sherwood.
