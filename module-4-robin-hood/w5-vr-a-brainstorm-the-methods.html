📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
How Does Robin Get Justice Done?
A brainstorm board on the morality of his methods
This week's chapters (12 to 14) show Robin and his men choosing whom to help and how far to go. Little John goes after the Sheriff, Robin recruits a tanner by fighting him, and Robin breaks up a forced wedding to marry a minstrel to the woman he loves. Robin takes from the rich and gives to the poor, but he also robs travelers and disrupts a lawful ceremony. This is a brainstorm board, not a finished page. You open a board and lay out, in your own words, how Robin gets justice done and whether his methods are right. The notes you make here become evidence for your "Portrait of a Hero" essay.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
When Robin takes the law into his own hands, do his methods serve justice, break it, or both?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place and move notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your reading notes on Chapters 12 to 14 and your Hero-versus-Outlaw evidence chart from your Reading Journal.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a board and place a note at the top with the focus question: when Robin takes the law into his own hands, do his methods serve justice, break it, or both?
2
Add three method notes from Chapters 12 to 14, one per note: Little John outwitting the Sheriff as a beggar, Robin recruiting the tanner Arthur-a-Bland by fighting him, and Robin breaking up the forced wedding to marry Allin-a-Dale to his true love. For each, write in one phrase what Robin or his men actually do.
3
Under each method note, add a small note that answers: does this method serve justice or break the law, or both? Be honest where it is both. Disrupting a wedding helps two people and breaks a lawful ceremony at the same time.
4
At the bottom, add one note with your early answer to the focus question in a single sentence. This is not your final thesis. It is your first honest guess, and you can change it.
5
Take a screenshot of your brainstorm board. Submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 4.5A: Brainstorm Robin's Methods. In VR 4.5B you move these notes onto your growing Hero-versus-Outlaw evidence board. (No headset? Lay the same notes out on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board has three method notes from Chapters 12 to 14, each saying what Robin or his men actually do.
☐
Each method has a note saying whether it serves justice, breaks the law, or both.
☐
I added one early-answer sentence and submitted a screenshot to VR 4.5A.
