📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Finish the Board, Then Pick a Side
The completed Hero-versus-Outlaw evidence board
This week you finish the evidence board you have grown all module and make a decision: is Robin a hero, an outlaw, or both? You have read most of the book, you have weighed his methods, and you brainstormed a thesis in VR 4.6A. Now you complete the board, weigh the two sides honestly, and commit to the position your essay will defend. A finished board with a chosen position turns the rest of the essay into building and explaining, not deciding.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
With the whole board in front of you, what is your position, and what is the single best piece of evidence for the other side that you will have to answer?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: the same evidence board you have been growing, with a Hero side and an Outlaw side, where you can place and move notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your full evidence board, your circled thesis from VR 4.6A, and any notes from Chapters 15 to 17.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your full evidence board. Add any last notes from Chapters 15 to 17 to the side they fit, so the board now covers the whole book up to this point. Make sure every note has a chapter and page number.
2
Stand back and read both sides. Move your three strongest notes to the top of each side so the best evidence is easy to find. Set the weaker notes lower.
3
Place a clear POSITION note at the center of the board: hero, outlaw, or both. If you choose both, your note must say which side matters more and why. This is the position your essay will defend.
4
Mark the single best piece of evidence for the other side with a different color. That is the point you will have to answer fairly in your essay's concession. Naming it now makes your argument honest and stronger.
5
Take a screenshot of your finished board with your position note and your marked other-side evidence. Submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 4.6B: Finish the Board. Next week you turn this into your essay plan and start building the Forest Court. (No headset? Finish the same two-sided chart on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board is finished, covers the book so far, and has its strongest notes moved to the top of each side.
☐
I placed a clear position note (hero, outlaw, or both, with which matters more) at the center.
☐
I marked the best other-side evidence and submitted a screenshot to VR 4.6B.
