📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
The Adventures of Robin Hood
Roger Lancelyn Green · building toward your essay
Open the same Hero or Outlaw? board you have been growing since Week 1. This week you add the Week 3 evidence from Chapters 6–8, sorting the new details into your HERO and OUTLAW columns. The board is a tool for weighing a question across a whole book. As the HERO column fills with Robin's code and his help to the poor, watch how the balance shifts for your essay "Portrait of a Hero".
Keep one board for the whole module. Each week you add to it, so by the time you write, the evidence is already gathered and sorted.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
After Chapters 6–8, does the HERO column now hold your strongest single piece of evidence?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: the running two-column board from VR 4.1B and 4.2B. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Week 3 brainstorm board from VR 4.3A and your own copy of the book.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your running Hero or Outlaw? board with its HERO and OUTLAW columns and your Week 1 and Week 2 notes. Add a small "Week 3" label so the new evidence is easy to find.
2
Add at least two new notes to the HERO column (for example, Robin's rule about whom not to rob, or the loan that saves Sir Richard's lands). For each, write one line on why it shows a sense of justice.
3
Add at least one new note to the OUTLAW column (for example, the money Robin gives away is still money his band took from travelers). Note honestly whether this weakens the hero reading or not.
4
Put a star next to the single note you think is your strongest evidence so far, on either side. A good essay leads with its strongest evidence, so marking it now saves you time when you write.
5
Take a screenshot of your updated board. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 4.3B: Add to the Board. (No headset? Build the same chart on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
I added Week 3 evidence to the same two-column board I have been growing since Week 1.
☐
I added at least three new details from Chapters 6–8 across the two columns, each with a reason.
☐
I starred my strongest piece of evidence and submitted a screenshot to VR 4.3B.
