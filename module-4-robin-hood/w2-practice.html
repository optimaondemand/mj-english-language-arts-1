📚 GRAMMAR · SIMPLE & COMPOUND SENTENCES
A quick reminder: a compound sentence joins two complete thoughts with a comma plus a joining word like and, but, or so. Leaving out the joining word, or using only a comma, makes a run-on. Rate each statement below.
⚡ CHECK YOURSELF · TRUE OR NOT?
For each statement, choose how true it is. Pick one and you'll get instant feedback.
1. "A compound sentence joins two complete thoughts with a comma and a joining word."
2. "Joining two complete thoughts with only a comma and no joining word is a correctly formed compound sentence."
3. "Just adding the word 'and' between two complete thoughts always fixes a run-on."

⚡ THINK, THEN REVEAL

In your own words, what does tithe mean in Robin's world?
Reveal to check
A tithe is a tenth part taken as a payment or fine, often a harsh burden on the poor. The Sheriff's men squeeze a harsh tithe from Robin's old tenants.
What does it mean when Lord Fitzwalter renounces Robin?
Reveal to check
To renounce is to give up or formally reject someone or something. Lord Fitzwalter renounces Robin, cutting off ties with him.
Why would being called valiant matter to Robin's new band?
Reveal to check
Valiant means brave and determined. Green describes the "hundred valiant men" who join Robin, a word that shows the band earns respect through courage, not just numbers.
