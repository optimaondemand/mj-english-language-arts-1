The Adventures of Robin Hood

Open the same Hero or Outlaw? board you have grown for four weeks. You add the Week 4 evidence from Chapters 9–11, then use the whole board to do something new: draft a first thesis, one sentence that answers the module question. This is the same board from Weeks 1 to 3. It is now full enough to argue from, which is exactly what it was built to do.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Based on four weeks of evidence, is Robin a hero or an outlaw, and how do justice and law come apart in the book?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: the running two-column board from VR 4.1B, 4.2B, and 4.3B. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Week 4 brainstorm board from VR 4.4A and your own copy of the book.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your running Hero or Outlaw? board with all your Week 1–3 notes. Add a small "Week 4" label for the new evidence.
2
Add at least two new notes from Chapters 9–11 to the columns where they belong (for example, the honest repayment of the loan in the HERO column, or robbing travelers on the highway in the OUTLAW column). Add one line of reasoning to each.
3
Stand back and read the whole board. Make a new note titled "My position" and write one sentence answering the module question: is Robin a hero or an outlaw, and what does the book show about justice and law? This is a first draft of your thesis, and you can revise it later.
4
Point to one note in the opposite column from your position and label it "concession". A strong essay admits the best point on the other side before answering it. Knowing your concession now will make the essay honest and stronger.
5
Take a screenshot of your full board with your position and concession marked. (No headset? Build the same chart on paper or in a slide.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
I added Week 4 evidence from Chapters 9–11 to the same board I have grown all module.
☐
I wrote a one-sentence "My position" note answering the module question.
☐
I marked one note as my "concession".
