📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
The Adventures of Robin Hood
Roger Lancelyn Green · building toward your essay
Open the same Hero or Outlaw? board you started in Week 1. This week you add the Week 2 evidence to it, sorting new details from Chapters 3–5 into your HERO and OUTLAW columns. The board is a tool for weighing a question over time, the way a running chart is. As it fills, you can see which way the evidence leans for your essay "Portrait of a Hero".
Keep one board for the whole module. Each week you add to it, so by the time you write, the evidence is already gathered and sorted.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
After Chapters 3–5, which way is your evidence leaning, and which column is harder to fill?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: the two-column board you set up in VR 4.1B. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Week 2 brainstorm board from VR 4.2A and your own copy of the book.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your running Hero or Outlaw? board, the one with the HERO column and the OUTLAW column from Week 1. Add a small "Week 2" label so you can see which evidence came from which reading.
2
From your brainstorm, add at least two new notes to the HERO column (for example, rescuing Will Scarlet from the Sheriff's men, or welcoming Little John fairly after losing the duel). For each, write one line on why it counts as heroic.
3
Add at least one new note to the OUTLAW column (for example, Robin now lives outside the law in the forest and his men take from travelers). Note whether this is a fair charge once you see why he is there.
4
Step back and add a "leaning so far" note at the bottom: based on two weeks of evidence, which side is your board pointing toward, and which column was harder to fill? This is a first guess at your essay's position, and you can change it as the book goes on.
5
Take a screenshot of your updated board. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 4.2B: Add to the Board. (No headset? Build the same chart on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
I added Week 2 evidence to the same two-column board I started in Week 1.
☐
I added at least three new details from Chapters 3–5 across the two columns, each with a reason.
☐
I added a "leaning so far" note and submitted a screenshot to VR 4.2B.
