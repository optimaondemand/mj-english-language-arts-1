Week 6 · Workshop Day
From Evidence to a Working Thesis
This is your studio day. No new reading. You write, you build in VR, and you draft the claim your essay will argue.
📝 WHAT TODAY IS FOR
You now have two full weeks of evidence on whether Robin is a hero or an outlaw. Today you turn that evidence into a working thesis, the single sentence your essay will defend. Work through the three stops below in order, then submit your Reading Journal for this module's Reading Journal Check. Each stop tells you exactly where to go in Canvas.
⚡ CHECK IN
Look over your evidence chart. Which side has more weight for you now? (No wrong answer. This just helps you draft your claim.)
📝 STOP 1 · QUICK WRITE 6 (graded)
This week's write is Quick Write 6: Draft Your Working Thesis. Using your evidence chart, write one arguable sentence that answers the essential question, then add a genuine concession that admits the other side. Write it in Microsoft Word and submit via the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab. Full directions and the M365 reminder are on the Canvas assignment.
➡️ Go to Quick Write 6 · [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
🧐 STOP 2 · VR ACTIVITY (completion)
Use VR this week as a thinking-and-building tool: brainstorm your thesis and arrange your strongest evidence, then build toward your "Portrait of a Hero" essay. The VR page has the full steps.
🧐 Note: Optima's VR platform is being upgraded. Your facilitator will confirm how to open this activity in the current app. The thinking and the thing you build are what matter, and they transfer to any tool.
➡️ Open this week's VR activity · [Teacher: link the VR page]
📚 STOP 3 · MODULE PROJECT · ONE STEP
Your end-of-module project is the "Portrait of a Hero" essay, due at the end of Week 8. This week's brick: in your Reading Journal, write your working thesis at the top of a new "Essay Plan" section, then list the three pieces of evidence you will build your body paragraphs around. Next week you will draft those paragraphs.
➡️ Open your Reading Journal · [Teacher: link the journal setup page]
📁 READING JOURNAL CHECK (completion-graded)
It has been two weeks, so this is a Reading Journal Check. Submit your Reading Journal Word doc through the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab so your facilitator can see your Week 5 and Week 6 notes, your evidence chart, and your new working thesis. This is completion-graded: the points are for keeping up your journal, not for being perfect.
➡️ Go to the Reading Journal Check · [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
💪 YOU'RE DONE WHEN
You've submitted Quick Write 6, recorded your VR brainstorm, written your working thesis in your journal, and turned in your Reading Journal Check. That's a full week, and your essay now has a backbone. Nicely done.
