📚 GRAMMAR · QUOTING & CITING EVIDENCE
A quick reminder: a quotation needs a signal phrase, correct punctuation, and a page citation. Flip each card to check yourself.
⚡ TAP TO CHECK · QUOTE IT RIGHT
Answer in your head before you flip. Then check yourself.
How do you introduce a quotation so it doesn't just drop in? (Say your answer, then tap to flip)
Use a signal phrase that names who is speaking or acting, like "Green writes," followed by a comma and the quotation.
Where does the page number go when you cite a quotation? (Say your answer, then tap to flip)
In parentheses right after the closing quotation mark, with the sentence's final period placed after the parenthesis: …"(142).
What is a "dropped quote," and why is it a problem? (Say your answer, then tap to flip)
A dropped quote has no signal phrase and no citation, so the reader cannot tell who said it or check where it comes from in the book.
📚 VOCABULARY · WORDS OF CRUSADE AND DISGUISE

⚡ THINK, THEN REVEAL
Write your own answer first, then open the panel to compare. There is no auto-grading here. The thinking is the point.
The king disguises himself as a "tall palmer." In your own words, what is a palmer?
Reveal to check
A palmer is a pilgrim who has traveled to the Holy Land. It's a clever disguise because a palmer's plain dress and constant traveling make him unremarkable.
What does King Richard's pardon actually do for Robin?
Reveal to check
A pardon is an official decision to forgive a crime and cancel its punishment. Richard's pardon clears the outlaw charge against Robin.
Why is reconciliation the right word for what happens between the king and Robin?
Reveal to check
Reconciliation means making peace and restoring a broken relationship. That is exactly what happens when the rightful king and the outlaw make friends again.
