Lesson 4.16
Making the Verb Agree with Its Subject
Grammar & Vocabulary · Week 3
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A singular subject takes a singular verb; a plural subject takes a plural verb.
You'll learn to match a verb to its subject in number, and to keep matching even when other words sit between them. Then a short quiz checks what you've got. Writing about a whole band of outlaws is the perfect place to practice, because subjects switch between one person and many.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to match a verb to a singular or plural subject.
2
I will be able to find the true subject when a phrase comes between the subject and the verb.
⚡ WARM-UP
Quick gut check: which sounds right to your ear, "The band of outlaws lives in Sherwood" or "The band of outlaws live in Sherwood"? (No wrong answer yet.)
📚 THE LESSON

Every sentence has a subject (who or what the sentence is about) and a verb (the action or state). Subject–verb agreement means the verb must match the subject in number. One person or thing takes a singular verb: "Robin shoots well." More than one takes a plural verb: "The outlaws shoot well." Notice that the singular verb often ends in s while the plural one does not, which feels backward but is the usual rule. The trap to watch for is a phrase that slips in between the subject and the verb. In "The leader of the outlaws is brave," the subject is "leader," not "outlaws," so the verb is the singular "is." To find the true subject, cross out the in-between phrase and ask who or what is really doing the action. One more useful case: collective nouns like "band," "company," and "court" name a group but count as one unit, so they usually take a singular verb. As you write about Robin and his men this week, keep checking: one or many, and which word is the true subject.

⚡ TRY IT · MAKE IT AGREE
Choose the verb that agrees with the subject. Pick one and you'll get instant feedback.
1. The outlaws ______ Lincoln green in the forest.
2. The leader of the outlaws ______ brave and fair.
3. Robin's band ______ in the greenwood each morning.
4. Friar Tuck ______ the band after the fight by the river.
