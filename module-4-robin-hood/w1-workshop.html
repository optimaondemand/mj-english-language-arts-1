Put the Week to Work
This is your studio day. No new reading. You write, you build in VR, and you take the first step on the module essay.
📝 WHAT TODAY IS FOR
You've met Robin Hood, watched him outlawed on a lie, and studied the words and craft of his world. Today you turn that into something of your own: a short write, a quick piece of real research, a brainstorm you build in VR, and the first brick of your module essay. Work through the four stops below in order. Each one tells you exactly where to go in Canvas.
⚡ CHECK IN
How is your thinking feeling after Week 1? (No wrong answer. This just helps you pick where to slow down.)
📝 STOP 1 · QUICK WRITE 1 (graded)
This week's write is Quick Write 1: Hero or Outlaw? First Take. In a short paragraph, take a first position on the module question (is Robin a hero or an outlaw?) and back it with one moment from the Prologue or Chapters 1–2. Write it in Microsoft Word and submit via the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab. Full directions and the M365 steps are on the Canvas assignment. This is the module's first graded write, so the assignment carries the full M365 Skill Step box.
➡️ Go to Quick Write 1 · [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
🔍 STOP 2 · MINI-RESEARCH TASK (light rubric)
Robin Hood is a legend people have argued about for centuries: was there a real outlaw behind the name? Find two reliable sources (an encyclopedia article, a museum or university site — not a random blog or AI answer) that discuss whether a historical Robin Hood existed, then write a short paragraph on what they agree or disagree about. Write it in Microsoft Word and submit via the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab. Full directions are on the Canvas assignment.
➡️ Go to the Mini-Research Task · [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
🧐 STOP 3 · VR ACTIVITY (completion)
Use VR this week to brainstorm, then build toward your "Portrait of a Hero" essay: gather the traits and moments that will become your evidence about Robin. The VR pages have the full steps.
🧐 Note: Optima's VR platform is being upgraded. Your facilitator will confirm how to open this activity in the current app. The thinking and the thing you build are what matter, and they transfer to any tool.
➡️ Open this week's VR activity · [Teacher: link the VR page]
📚 STOP 4 · MODULE PROJECT · ONE STEP
Your end-of-module project is the "Portrait of a Hero" essay, which answers: is Robin Hood a hero or an outlaw, and what happens when justice and the law are not the same thing? This week's brick: in your Reading Journal (the Word doc in your OneDrive ELA6 folder), make sure your Hero-or-Outlaw chart has at least one moment under each heading from this week's reading. Small steps now make the final week easy.
➡️ Open your Reading Journal · [Teacher: link the journal setup page]
💪 YOU'RE DONE WHEN
You've submitted Quick Write 1, submitted the Mini-Research Task, recorded your VR activity, and filled in your Hero-or-Outlaw chart for Week 1. That's a full week. Nicely done.
