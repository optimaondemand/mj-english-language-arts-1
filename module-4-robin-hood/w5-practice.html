📚 GRAMMAR · COMPLEX SENTENCES
A quick reminder: a complex sentence joins an independent clause (can stand alone) to a dependent clause (cannot stand alone, thanks to a starter word like because, when, or although). Try the three checks below.
⚡ TRY IT · BUILD AND PUNCTUATE
Answer each row. Pick one and you'll get instant feedback.
1. Which is a dependent clause?
2. Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
3. Which is a complex sentence?
