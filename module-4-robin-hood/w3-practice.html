📚 GRAMMAR · SUBJECT–VERB AGREEMENT
A quick reminder: the verb must match its subject in number. Watch for a phrase that slips in between the subject and the verb. Flip each card to try one.
⚡ TAP TO CHECK · DOES IT AGREE?
Say your answer out loud (or in your head) before you flip. Then check yourself.
Robin's band ______ in the greenwood each morning. (meets / meet) (Say your answer, then tap to flip)
meets. "Band" is a collective noun that names one group, so it takes the singular verb "meets."
The leader of the outlaws ______ brave and fair. (is / are) (Say your answer, then tap to flip)
is. Cross out "of the outlaws." The true subject is the singular "leader," so the singular verb "is" agrees.
Two archers from the band ______ guard at the camp tonight. (stands / stand) (Say your answer, then tap to flip)
stand. The subject is the plural "archers," so the plural verb "stand" agrees, even though "band" sits nearby.
