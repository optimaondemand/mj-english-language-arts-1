💡 EXTRA PRACTICE · OPTIONAL AND UNGRADED
This is optional extra practice. Nothing here is graded. Use it if you want more reps on this week's grammar and vocabulary: comparison/contrast transitions, the periodic sentence.
⚡ ROUND 1 · WORDS FOR COMPARISON & ARGUMENT · FLIP EACH CARD
Read the word, guess its meaning, then click the card to check yourself.
claim
Click to reveal
A statement you argue for, one a reader could reasonably dispute. Example: "My claim is that real courage always costs the hero something."
evidence
Click to reveal
The specific moment from a text that supports a claim. Example: "The strongest evidence is the scene where Prospero forgives his brother."
parallel
Click to reveal
A likeness between two things that run alongside each other. Example: "There is a clear parallel between Prospero's mercy and Daniel's."
synthesis
Click to reveal
Putting separate pieces together into one larger idea. Example: "The synthesis names the one truth all three characters share."
⚡ ROUND 2 · TRANSITIONS & THE PERIODIC SENTENCE
Answer each row. Pick one and you'll get instant feedback.
1. "Ariel serves Prospero faithfully. ______, Caliban resents every task he is given." Which transition fits?
2. "Prospero forgives his enemies. ______, the king of Naples repents his own wrongdoing." Which transition fits?
3. Which is the periodic sentence (it saves its main point for the end)?
