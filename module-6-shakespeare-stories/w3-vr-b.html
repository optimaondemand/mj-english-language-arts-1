📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
What I Carry Forward
The spoken half of your year capstone
This is the second half of your "What Lasts" capstone, and it is personal. Your essay makes an argument. This recording answers a different, simpler question: of everything you read this year, what is one thing you carry forward, and which character helped you see it? Speaking it out loud makes you say plainly what the year gave you. You record it now, and it submits together with your essay as one project.
Your reflection does not have to match your essay. The essay is your argument. This is what the year meant to you.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What is one thing you are carrying forward from this year's reading, and which character helped you see it?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset, or any voice recorder if VR is unavailable
Activity type: solo. No live session, no scheduled time, no classmates present.
Tool you'll use: a voice recorder. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your "What Lasts" board, your Reading Journal, and a moment from the one character you want to talk about.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your VR app on your own. Decide on the one thing you are carrying forward and the one character who helped you see it. Say it once out loud to hear how it sounds before you record.
2
Make a voice recording (2–3 minutes). A short screen or video recording also works. In it: (a) name the one thing you carry forward; (b) describe the character who helped you see it, with one moment from the book; (c) say why it matters to you now.
3
Listen back once. Check that all three parts are there and that it sounds like you, not a book report. If a part is missing or rushed, record it again.
4
Save your recording. You will submit it together with your comparative essay in the Canvas assignment "What Lasts" Module Project. This recording is Part 2 of that one project, not a separate submission. Then finish and submit your essay in Word. (No headset? Record the same reflection with any voice recorder and submit the audio.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My recording (2–3 minutes) names one thing I carry forward, describes the character who helped me see it with one moment, and says why it matters now.
☐
It sounds personal and specific, not like a summary of the book.
☐
I submitted my recording together with my essay in the "What Lasts" Module Project.
