⚡ ROUND 2 · WORDS FROM MUCH ADO · THINK FIRST, THEN REVEAL
Decide on your answer in your head before you open each drop-down. Click the summary again to close it and try another guess.
1. Beatrice and Benedick's quick, witty, back-and-forth remarks to each other are called their ______.
Reveal the word
sallies — quick, clever, or witty remarks. Beatrice diverts Hero with her sprightly sallies.
2. Which word names false words spoken to ruin someone's good name?
Reveal the word
slander — false words spoken to damage someone's good name. The plot against Hero is built on slander.
3. Which word names an act done to make up for a wrong?
Reveal the word
penance — an act done to make up for a wrong. Claudio agrees to endure whatever penance Leonato asks.
4. Which word means good-humored teasing back and forth?
Reveal the word
raillery — good-humored teasing or joking. Beatrice and Benedick keep up a constant war of raillery.
