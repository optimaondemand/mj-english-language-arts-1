📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Tales from Shakespeare · "Much Ado About Nothing"
Charles & Mary Lamb · the prose retelling · read from your own copy
VR is your walk-around whiteboard again, a tool for thinking. Today you brainstorm the big ideas in "Much Ado About Nothing": the two kinds of deception (the kind trick that brings Beatrice and Benedick together, and Don John's cruel lie that nearly destroys Hero), the love hidden under all that wit, and the honor that is wounded by unproven words and then restored. The ideas you pull out feed your year capstone, the comparative essay and reflection called "What Lasts."
You are not visiting a ready-made world. You are building a board of your own ideas, using moments you found in the reading.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
In "Much Ado About Nothing," how can the same trick build a life or wreck one, and what finally lasts: a wound from words, or the truth that restores honor?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place notes and simple objects. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your own copy of the tale and your Reading Journal, open to your Week 2 notes.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a blank board (or clear space). Title it Much Ado: Big Ideas. Across the top, place four idea notes: Deception, Love, Wit, and Honor.
2
Under Deception, make a kind trick note and a cruel trick note: the prince's plan to let Benedick and Beatrice overhear that each is loved, beside Don John paying Borachio to stage Margaret at Hero's window to deceive Claudio. Add one phrase under each saying what it built or wrecked.
3
Under Love and Wit, place two or three moments: Beatrice and Benedick's "war of raillery"; Benedick deciding to "requite her love"; Beatrice answering "Kill Claudio" when Benedick offers to do anything for her. Notice how the wit hides real feeling.
4
Under Honor, place two moments: Hero shamed at the altar on hearsay and swooning as if dead; the truth surfacing when Borachio is caught, and Claudio doing honest penance before Hero is restored. Use the moments you actually noticed.
5
Add one answer note at the bottom: in one sentence, say what the tale shows lasts when honor is wounded by words. Then take a screenshot and submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 6.2A: Brainstorm Board. (No headset? Build the same board on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
