Where joins go wrong in your own writing
Professor Lovelore At the board
0:56
An AI-generated character and voice.
The comma splice
Launcelot was the best knight, he could not achieve the Grail.→Launcelot was the best knight, but he could not achieve the Grail.
The leaning part left alone
Because his heart was divided.→Because his heart was divided, Launcelot could not achieve the quest.
However is not a joining word
Launcelot was the greatest knight in arms, however he did not achieve the Grail.→Launcelot was the greatest knight in arms; however, he did not achieve the Grail.

Three lenses for your draft
  1. Joins. And, but, or, so, for, nor and yet after a comma are marked. A comma that may be holding two thoughts alone is flagged.
  2. Leaning Parts. Because, when, although, before, after, since and while are marked. A leaning part with no comma, or with no main part, is flagged.
  3. Evidence. Each chapter you name and each quotation is marked.
