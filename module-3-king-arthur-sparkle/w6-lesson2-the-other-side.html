Part 1Evidence ThinkingComplete sentences, with moments from the book.
1. What is the strongest thing about the knight you argue is less ideal? Give the moment and its chapter.
2. Is your claim an either/or? Name a third possibility the book leaves open, or explain why there really are only two.
3. Green’s narrator says few knights “were so worthy as Gawain” (the end of “Sir Gawain and the Green Knight”), and Launcelot’s brother calls him “the most courteous knight that ever bore shield” (the Epilogue, “Avalon”). Pick one: does the book’s own praise help or hurt your claim?
