👋 WELCOME
This year you will read some of the great hero stories of the Western tradition — a Roman officer who faces a dragon, the knights of Camelot, an outlaw in Sherwood Forest, a young man in first-century Judea, and characters from Shakespeare — and you will join a very old conversation: What makes someone truly good? What is worth being brave for?
You will read closely, build a strong vocabulary, sharpen your grammar and writing, and tell what you have learned in your own words. By June you will be a more careful reader and a clearer writer — and you will have thought hard about courage, honesty, loyalty, and what it costs to do the right thing.

📝 HOW THIS COURSE WORKS
This is a self-paced course. You move through each week's lesson on your own, in Canvas. A facilitator grades your work, answers your questions by email and in the Canvas Inbox, and holds office hours — reach out any time you are stuck.
Each week you can expect a reading, a lesson page with close-reading and grammar work, a short quiz, a Quick Write, sometimes a discussion or a VR activity, and steady progress toward each module's project. Most of your writing is done in Microsoft Word and turned in through Canvas.
🚀 START HERE (WEEK 1)
  1. Read the M365 Setup & File Organization Guide (pinned in every module).
  2. Complete Lesson 0.1 — Digital Binder Setup to build your OneDrive folder.
  3. Complete Lesson 0.2 — Reading Journal in M365 to start your journal.
  4. Take the Module 1 Intro survey and begin Week 1.
💬 NEED HELP?
Message your facilitator through the Canvas Inbox (the envelope icon). For Microsoft 365 or OneDrive trouble, see the M365 guide's help section or visit support.microsoft.com. You are never on your own here — ask early.
