📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
The Bronze Bow
Elizabeth George Speare · building toward your essay
This is the last time you build the board, and it is the most important. You add your Week 3 choices to the hatred-to-love line and step back to read the whole path at once. The finished line is your evidence map: it shows where Daniel started, what pulled him in each direction, and the turning points that carried him toward love. From this map you will pull the thesis and the body paragraphs of your essay "The Power of Choice: Daniel's Journey."
The completed line answers the module's question: what changes Daniel. You will write your essay straight from it.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Reading the whole line, what is the one thing that moves Daniel from hatred toward love?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place labeled notes along a line and add a short voice note. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Daniel's Choices board with the line from Weeks 1 and 2, your VR 5.3A notes, and your own copy of the book.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your Daniel's Choices board and find the hatred-to-love line. You will finish the same line, not start a new one.
2
Take three or four choices from your VR 5.3A notes and place each as a marker on the line. The break with Rosh and rejecting "It's not my affair" move sharply toward love. The deaths in the rescue and the rage at Leah show the cost and may pull back toward hatred. Place each where the choice itself leans.
3
Mark the biggest turning point on the whole line with a star or a brighter note. Most readers choose Daniel's talk with Jesus, where he hears "the only thing stronger than hate is love." In one line, say why this is the hinge of his change.
4
Stand back, read the line from left to right, and add a short voice note (about 30 seconds) or a written note answering the focus question: what is the one thing that moves Daniel toward love? This sentence is the seed of your essay thesis.
5
Take a screenshot of the finished line and save your voice note. You'll submit them in the Canvas assignment VR 5.3B: Finish the Board. (No headset? Finish the same line on paper or in a slide, write your thesis sentence, and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My line now holds choices from all three weeks, and I marked the biggest turning point.
☐
I recorded or wrote a one-sentence answer to "what moves Daniel toward love," my essay thesis seed.
☐
I submitted a screenshot of the finished line (and my voice note) to VR 5.3B.
