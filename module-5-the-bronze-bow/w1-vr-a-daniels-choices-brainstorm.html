📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
The Bronze Bow
Elizabeth George Speare · Chapters 1–8 · read from your own copy
Think of VR here as a giant whiteboard you can walk around in. It is a tool for thinking, the way a word processor is a tool for writing. Today you start a workspace you will keep all module long: your Daniel's Choices board. You will gather the moments in Chapters 1 through 8 where Daniel makes a choice, and what drives that choice. Everything you collect feeds your module project, the essay "The Power of Choice: Daniel's Journey," which traces how Daniel moves from hatred toward love.
You are not visiting a ready-made world. You are building a board of your own evidence, using moments you found in the reading.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
In the opening chapters, what does Daniel choose, and what is driving him to choose it?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place notes and simple objects. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your own copy of the book and your Reading Journal (the Word doc you set up in the Reading Journal lesson), open to your Week 1 notes.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a blank board (or clear space) in your VR app. Title it Daniel's Choices. This is your workspace for the whole module.
2
Make a note for the question this week is testing: what is driving Daniel's choices so far? Write it in one sentence so you can answer it with evidence below.
3
Make four to six choice notes, one moment each, from Chapters 1 through 8: Daniel joining Rosh's band to ambush the caravan and free the slave; filing the chains off Samson; refusing to give the Roman soldier's horse water; hiding in the secret passage at Hezron's house; telling the story of his father's crucifixion in the passageway; swearing the oath "For God's Victory" and taking the bronze bow as the band's sign; watching Jesus heal the boy's withered hand. Use the moments you actually noticed.
4
Add one or two "what drives him" notes beside each choice. For example, the vow Daniel makes after his father's death shows that hatred and the wish for revenge are driving him. Naming the feeling behind each choice is the start of your evidence for what changes Daniel later.
5
Take a screenshot of your finished board. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 5.1A: Brainstorm Board. (No headset? Build the same board on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board names this week's question and has four to six real choice moments from Chapters 1 through 8.
☐
I named one or two things driving each choice, starting my evidence for what moves Daniel.
☐
I submitted a screenshot of my board to VR 5.1A.
