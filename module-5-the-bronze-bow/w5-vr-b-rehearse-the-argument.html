📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Say It Out Loud Before You Write It
The spoken rehearsal of your essay on Daniel's journey
Speaking an argument out loud tests whether it holds together. You will record a short spoken version of your essay: state your claim about what changes Daniel, walk through his early hatred and the turning points, give the love-and-forgiveness evidence, and close with what the journey means. As you speak, lean on the three ways to persuade you studied this week: reason (logos), trust in the speaker (ethos), and feeling (pathos). Then you write the full essay in Word, using what you heard yourself say.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Said out loud, does your argument actually prove what changes Daniel, and does it use reason, trust, and feeling to persuade?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset, or any voice recorder if VR is unavailable
Activity type: solo. No live session, no scheduled time, no classmates present.
Tool you'll use: a voice recorder. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your essay plan from VR 5.5A, with your claim, early hatred, turning points, love-and-forgiveness evidence, and what the journey means.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your VR app on your own. Out loud, rehearse once from your plan: state your claim, then walk through Daniel's early hatred, his turning points, and his love and forgiveness.
2
Make a voice recording (2–3 minutes). A short screen or video recording also works. In it: (a) state your claim about what changes Daniel; (b) give his early hatred with one moment; (c) give a turning point with one moment; (d) give his love and forgiveness with one moment (the open door for Marcus is a strong one); (e) close with what the journey means. As you go, try to use reason, trust, and feeling to make the case land.
3
Listen back once. Note the one spot that sounded weakest out loud. That is the part of your essay to strengthen when you write the final draft.
4
Save your recording. Submit it to the Canvas assignment VR 5.5B: Rehearse Your Argument with a one-sentence note on what you found hardest to say out loud. Then write your full essay in Word for the module project.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My recording (2–3 minutes) states my claim, then walks through early hatred, a turning point, and love and forgiveness with one moment each.
☐
I closed with what Daniel's journey means, and I tried to use reason, trust, and feeling to persuade.
☐
