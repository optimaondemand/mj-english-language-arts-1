Lesson 5.23
The Words of Healing and Home
Grammar & Vocabulary · Week 4
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
The last chapter trades the words of war for the words of home.
This week's words come from Chapter 24: sickness and healing, trust and its loss, and the marks of a home. You'll meet them where they live in the chapter. Reading a word inside a real sentence lets you use the surrounding words to figure out its meaning. The craft term for those surrounding hints is context clues. Then you'll make one word your own by rewriting a sentence three ways.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use this week's words correctly in the context of the story.
2
I will be able to take one sentence and rewrite it three ways to make a word my own.
⚡ WARM-UP · FLIP TO CHECK
One of this week's words is mezuzah. Make a guess at its meaning, then flip the card.
📎 What is a mezuzah? (Tap to flip.)
A mezuzah is a small case holding a slip of Scripture, fixed to the doorpost of a Jewish home; people often touch it as they pass through the door. In Chapter 24 the mezuzah marks the threshold, the very doorway Daniel finally opens to the enemy. Watch how a word about a doorway becomes part of the ending.
📚 THIS WEEK'S WORDS, WHERE THEY LIVE

Every word below belongs to Chapter 24. Some name sickness and care (the fever, the doctor who despairs). Others name the inner turns of the ending (trust broken, peace found, a vow renewed). Watch for them as you finish the novel.

WordWhat it means here
physiciana doctor; one who treats the sick (the physician shakes his head over Leah)
betrayedlet down or harmed by someone trusted; "Leah was betrayed by the one thing she still reached out to"
mezuzaha small case of Scripture fixed to a doorpost of a Jewish home, touched as one passes
vigila period of staying awake to watch over someone (Marcus keeps a vigil across the road)
thresholdthe doorway or entrance of a house; the line Daniel forbids Marcus to cross, then opens
peacecalm and freedom from inner turmoil; the "peace he had never imagined" that fills Daniel
vowa solemn promise; Daniel and Thacia make "a new vow together" at the end
kingdomthe rule Jesus preaches; a word Daniel slowly learns does not mean an army against Rome
✏️ THE WRITTEN PRACTICE · MAKE A WORD YOUR OWN
Pick one word from the table above. Find (or write) one sentence that uses it. Then rewrite that sentence three ways in the "Week-by-Week Notes" section of your Reading Journal (the Word doc you set up in the Week-1 Reading Journal lesson):
  1. Swap: replace the word with a synonym, a word that means almost the same thing. Does the meaning shift?
  2. Stretch: keep the word but add detail until the picture is sharper.
  3. Imitate: keep the sentence's shape but write about something in your own life.
Finish with one line: which version is strongest, and why?
📖 FROM THE BOOK
Hunt for two of this week's words inside Chapter 24. (Read from your own copy of the book.) Copy each full sentence into the "Week-by-Week Notes" section of your Reading Journal. One of them becomes the sentence you rewrite three ways above.
