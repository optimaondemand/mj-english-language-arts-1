🧐 STOP 2 · VR ACTIVITY (completion)
Two short VR moves finish your argument. First, plan your essay on a board: lay out your thesis, the three or four moments in Daniel's journey, and one example each of logos, ethos, and pathos, so the shape of the essay is clear in front of you. Then rehearse your argument aloud: in a sentence or two, say what Daniel's journey from hatred to love means, and answer the strongest objection to it. This is your essay, spoken aloud. A short voice recording or a screenshot of your board is all you submit.
