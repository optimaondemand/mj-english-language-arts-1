Grammar & Vocabulary · Optional
This is optional extra practice — nothing here is graded. Use it if you want more reps on this week's grammar and vocabulary.
⚡ GRAMMAR PRACTICE · TAP TO CHECK YOURSELF
Read each sentence and decide the answer. Then tap the card to check.
Which words are the appositive? "Thacia, Joel's clever twin sister, spoke first." (Tap to flip.)
"Joel's clever twin sister" renames Thacia and sits beside her name, set off by commas. That is the appositive.
Which word is the adjective, and what does it describe? "The stubborn miser refused to open his door." (Tap to flip.)
"Stubborn" describes "miser." It is an adjective, telling us what kind of miser he is.
Is this appositive punctuated correctly? "Marcus a Roman soldier grew fond of Leah." (Tap to flip.)
No, it is missing commas. Correct: "Marcus, a Roman soldier, grew fond of Leah." An appositive that adds extra information needs a comma on each side.
