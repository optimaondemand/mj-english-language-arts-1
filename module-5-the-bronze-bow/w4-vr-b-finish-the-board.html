📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
From the Vow of Hatred to the Open Door
The finished Daniel's Choices board, plus your thesis
Since Week 1 you have been building a Daniel's Choices board, laying his choices along a line from hatred on one end to love on the other. Now that the book is finished, you complete it and read it as one whole. When you can see the full path from his vow of hatred to inviting Marcus in, you can decide what you think the book argues. That decision becomes your thesis: the one sentence your essay will prove about what changes Daniel.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Looking at his whole path from hatred to love, what actually changes Daniel, and what does that say about hatred and love?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: the same board you have been growing, where you can move and line up notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Daniel's Choices board (Weeks 1–3) and the Chapter 24 brainstorm from VR 5.4A.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your Daniel's Choices board. Lay it as one line, with hatred at one end and love at the other. Place his choices along the line in story order: the vow of hatred as a boy, sparing the old miser, taking in Leah, the break with Rosh, the cost when Samson and Nathan die, the talk with Jesus, the low point after the crown is refused, and finally the open door for Marcus.
2
Fill any gaps from this week's reading so the line is complete. Drop in your VR 5.4A notes on Chapter 24 at the love end.
3
Mark the two or three turning points where Daniel moves toward love (for example, the break with Rosh, the talk with Jesus, the healing of Leah). Put a clear mark on the board next to each one.
4
Now write your thesis on one note: in one sentence, say what finally changes Daniel and what the book argues about hatred and love. Push past "Daniel changes." A strong thesis claims an idea, for example that hatred imprisons the one who carries it while only love can open what hatred has shut.
5
Take a screenshot of your finished board with your thesis note on it. Submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 5.4B: Finish the Board. Next week you turn this board and thesis into your full essay plan. (No headset? Lay the same line out on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My Daniel's Choices board is complete, laid from hatred to love in story order.
☐
I marked two or three turning points where Daniel moves toward love.
☐
I wrote a one-sentence thesis that claims an idea (not just "Daniel changes"), then submitted a screenshot to VR 5.4B.
