📝 WHAT TODAY IS FOR
You've finished the novel. The bow has bent and the door is open. Today you turn all of it into the foundation of your essay: a thesis draft, a step you build in VR, and the biweekly Reading Journal Check. Work through the four stops below in order. Each one tells you exactly where to go in Canvas.
⚡ CHECK IN
How is your thinking feeling now that you've finished the book? (No wrong answer. This just helps you pick where to slow down.)
📝 STOP 1 · QUICK WRITE 4 (graded)
This week's write is Quick Write 4: Your Thesis Draft. You write a clear, arguable thesis for the module essay, list your three strongest pieces of evidence by chapter, and state the strongest objection to your own claim. Write it in Microsoft Word and submit via the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab. This is the direct foundation of your project essay, so keep the file. Full directions are on the Canvas assignment.
📁 M365 REMINDER: Name your file LastName_FirstName_QuickWrite4_ELA6, save to OneDrive → Optima → ELA → ELA6, and submit via the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab.

🧐 STOP 2 · VR ACTIVITY (completion, optional)
Use VR this week as a thinking-and-building tool: in your virtual space, build the AFTER side of Daniel's heart, the open door, and narrate how love finally won. This feeds the conclusion of your essay. A written or sketched version earns full credit. The VR page has the full steps.
🧐 Note: Optima's VR platform is being upgraded. Your facilitator will confirm how to open this activity in the current app. The thinking and the thing you build are what matter, and they transfer to any tool.

📋 STOP 3 · READING JOURNAL CHECK (completion)
Every two weeks you turn in your Reading Journal so your work is visible. This is the Week 4 Reading Journal Check. Make sure your "Week-by-Week Notes" section has your Week 3 and Week 4 entries: the break with Rosh, the cost of the sword, the line about hate and love, the quiet climax, the two meanings of the bronze bow, and the open door. Then submit the same Word doc via the Canvas Microsoft OneDrive tab. It is completion-graded, so full notes earn full credit.
➡️ Go to the Reading Journal Check · [Teacher: link the Canvas assignment]
📚 STOP 4 · MODULE PROJECT · ONE STEP
Your end-of-module project is the essay "The Power of Choice: Daniel's Journey." This week's brick: your Quick Write 4 thesis draft is the spine of that essay. In your Reading Journal, line up your three pieces of evidence in the order you'll argue them, from Daniel's vow of hatred to the open door. Next week you turn this into the full essay.
➡️ Open your Reading Journal · [Teacher: link the journal setup page]
💪 YOU'RE DONE WHEN
You've submitted Quick Write 4, recorded your VR activity (or its written version), turned in your Reading Journal for the check, and ordered your essay evidence. That's a full week, and the whole novel behind you. Next week you write the essay.
