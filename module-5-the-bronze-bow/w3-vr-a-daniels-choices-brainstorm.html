📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
The Bronze Bow
Elizabeth George Speare · Chapters 17–23 · read from your own copy
You are back at your Daniel's Choices board. This is the turning week. In Chapters 17 through 23 Daniel breaks with Rosh, leads the rescue, and pays a terrible cost for the sword. It is also where Jesus tells him plainly, "the only thing stronger than hate is love." Gather the choices that finally pull Daniel away from hatred. This is the heart of your essay "The Power of Choice: Daniel's Journey."
You are not visiting a ready-made world. You are adding to a board of your own evidence, using moments you found in the reading.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What choices in these chapters finally turn Daniel away from hatred, and what does each one cost him?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place notes and simple objects. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Daniel's Choices board from Weeks 1 and 2, your own copy of the book, and your Reading Journal open to your Week 3 notes.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your Daniel's Choices board. Add a new section titled Week 3 (Chapters 17–23) so this week's notes stay together.
2
Make a note for this week's question: what choices turn Daniel away from hatred, and what does each cost him? Write it in one sentence.
3
Make four to six choice notes, one moment each, from Chapters 17 through 23: Daniel begging Rosh to rescue the captured Joel, and Rosh refusing ("It's not my affair"); Daniel breaking with Rosh, "I am not one of your men. Not any longer"; leading the rescue in which Samson and Nathan die for him; carrying the guilt of the refrain "they who live by the sword will perish by the sword"; pouring out his guilt to Jesus, who reframes it ("It is hate that is the enemy. Not men"); raging at Leah over the soldier Marcus; rejecting Simon's faith after the feeding of the multitude and being left "with nothing but his hatred and his vow." Use the moments you actually noticed.
4
Add one or two "what drives him" notes beside each choice, and name the cost where there is one (the deaths of Samson and Nathan, Leah's collapse, his own loneliness). The costs are why Daniel's turn toward love is hard-won, which is exactly what your essay argues.
5
Take a screenshot of your Week 3 section. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 5.3A: Brainstorm Board. (No headset? Build the same notes on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board has a Week 3 section that names this week's question and has four to six real choice moments from Chapters 17 through 23.
☐
I named what drives each choice and the cost where there is one.
☐
I submitted a screenshot of my board to VR 5.3A.
