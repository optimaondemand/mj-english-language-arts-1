⚡ VOCAB PRACTICE · TAP EACH CARD TO CHECK YOURSELF
Guess the meaning of each word first. Then tap the card to check.
📎 thesis (tap to flip)
The main claim of an essay, the one debatable point you set out to prove.
📎 counterargument (tap to flip)
The strongest case against your claim, which you state fairly and then answer.
📎 evidence (tap to flip)
The facts and details from the text that support a claim.
📎 transformation (tap to flip)
A complete change in someone or something (Daniel's transformation from hatred to love).
📎 conviction (tap to flip)
A firm, deeply held belief.
📎 reconcile (tap to flip)
To make peace; to restore a friendly relationship after conflict.
