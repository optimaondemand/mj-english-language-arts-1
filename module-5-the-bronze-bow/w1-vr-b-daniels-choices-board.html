📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
The Bronze Bow
Elizabeth George Speare · building toward your essay
In VR 5.1A you gathered Daniel's choices. Now you arrange them on a line you will add to every week. Make a long line across your board. Label the far left end HATRED and the far right end LOVE. A line like this is a tool for tracking change, the same way a timeline tracks dates. You will place each of Daniel's choices along the line, closer to whichever end it leans toward. By Week 3 the line will show his whole path, and it becomes the backbone of your essay "The Power of Choice: Daniel's Journey."
Keep this board. It grows by a few choices each week and becomes the map of how Daniel changes.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Where on the line between hatred and love do Daniel's first choices fall?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can draw a line and place labeled notes along it. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your brainstorm board from VR 5.1A and your own copy of the book.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your Daniel's Choices board. Draw one long line across it. Label the left end HATRED and the right end LOVE. This is the line you will add to all module long.
2
Take three or four choices from your brainstorm board and place each one as a marker on the line, closer to whichever end it leans toward. The vow of hatred after his father's death sits far to the left. Filing the chains off Samson, who then attaches himself to Daniel, may sit a little nearer the middle.
3
Under each marker, add a one-line reason for where you placed it, naming what drives the choice. This is the level of detail your essay paragraphs will need.
4
Leave the right side of the line mostly empty for now. Most of Daniel's early choices lean toward hatred, and the move toward love comes later. Seeing the empty space tells you what to watch for in the coming weeks.
5
Take a screenshot of your line so far. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 5.1B: Build the Board. (No headset? Draw the same line on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board has a line labeled HATRED on the left and LOVE on the right.
☐
I placed three or four choices from Chapters 1 through 8 on the line, each with a one-line reason.
☐
I left room on the line for later choices and submitted a screenshot to VR 5.1B.
