📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Plan "The Power of Choice: Daniel's Journey"
The full plan for your essay on Daniel's transformation
This is essay week. You write the essay tracing Daniel's transformation from hatred toward love, and first you plan it. A board lets you see the whole essay at once and move pieces around before you commit them to Word. You will lay it out in order: your claim about what changes Daniel, his early hatred with evidence, the turning points, his love and forgiveness with evidence, and what the journey means. When the order looks right, writing it is mostly typing.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
In what order will your essay move from your claim to your conclusion, and where does each piece of evidence go?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place and reorder notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your finished Daniel's Choices board and your thesis note (from VR 5.4B) and your project file.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a board and lay out five labeled spots in a line, in this order: (1) your claim about what changes Daniel; (2) early hatred evidence; (3) the turning points; (4) love and forgiveness evidence; (5) what the journey means.
2
Drop in your evidence from the Daniel's Choices board. In spot 2 (early hatred) place his vow of hatred as a boy and Rosh's creed taking hold. In spot 4 (love and forgiveness) place the talk with Jesus, the healing of Leah, and the open door for Marcus. Under each piece, add one phrase saying how it shows the change.
3
Fill the turning points spot (3). Name the moments where Daniel actually shifts (the break with Rosh, the cost when Samson and Nathan die, the talk with Jesus). This is the hinge of your essay, where hatred starts giving way.
4
Walk the line once and reorder anything out of place. Make sure your claim is clear up front, your strongest evidence is not buried, and the ending says what Daniel's journey means rather than just repeating the claim.
5
Take a screenshot of your planned essay., then use it as your outline. Next you rehearse it aloud in VR 5.5B before you write the final essay in Word. (No headset? Lay the same plan out on paper or in a slide.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My plan has all five spots in order: claim, early hatred, turning points, love and forgiveness, and what the journey means.
☐
Each evidence spot holds real moments from the book, each with a phrase explaining what it shows.
☐
I have a screenshot of my plan.
