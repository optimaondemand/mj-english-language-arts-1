📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
The Bronze Bow
Elizabeth George Speare · building toward your essay
In VR 5.2A you gathered Daniel's Week 2 choices. Now you add them to the hatred-to-love line you drew in Week 1. The same line is your tool for tracking how Daniel changes. This week you will see something new: some choices, like caring for Leah and sparing the miser, sit closer to the love end, while joining the band's plans still pulls left. The pattern you are building becomes the backbone of your essay "The Power of Choice: Daniel's Journey."
Keep the same line. It is starting to show two pulls at once, which is the heart of what changes Daniel.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Which of Daniel's Week 2 choices lean toward love, and which still lean toward hatred?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place labeled notes along a line. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Daniel's Choices board with the line from Week 1, your VR 5.2A notes, and your own copy of the book.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your Daniel's Choices board and find the line you labeled HATRED on the left and LOVE on the right. You will add to the same line, not start a new one.
2
Take three or four choices from your VR 5.2A notes and place each as a new marker on the line. Place sparing the miser and caring for Leah toward the right. Place forming the band and following Rosh's cause more to the left.
3
Under each new marker, add a one-line reason for where you placed it, naming what drives the choice. Use a different color or shape for Week 2 markers so you can see your weeks apart.
4
Add one "the line is splitting" note somewhere on the board. In one sentence, say what you notice now that some choices lean toward love while others still lean toward hatred. This split is exactly what your essay will explain.
5
Take a screenshot of your updated line. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 5.2B: Add to the Board. (No headset? Update the same line on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
I added three or four Week 2 choices to the same hatred-to-love line, each with a one-line reason.
☐
My markers show some choices leaning toward love and some still toward hatred.
☐
I added a one-sentence note about the split and submitted a screenshot to VR 5.2B.
