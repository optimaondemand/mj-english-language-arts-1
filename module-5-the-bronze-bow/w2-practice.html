⚡ GRAMMAR PRACTICE · HOW STEADY IS THE TENSE?
For each sentence, rate how much the verb tense shifts. There is a best answer for each.
1. "Daniel worked at the forge and cared for Leah."
2. "Daniel works at the forge and cared for Leah."
3. "Daniel spared the miser, and now he works at the smithy."
⚡ VOCAB PRACTICE · GUESS, THEN CHECK
Before you open each box, guess what the word means. Then check yourself.
miser — what does it mean?
A person who hoards money and hates to spend it. In the novel, this is the old man Daniel is sent to rob.
parable — what does it mean?
A short, simple story that teaches a deeper truth. These are the kind of stories Jesus tells.
anvil — what does it mean?
A heavy iron block a blacksmith hammers metal against. Daniel beats out his anger on it.
conscience — what does it mean?
The inner sense of right and wrong that troubles Daniel after the robbery.
creed — what does it mean?
A set of beliefs a person lives by. Rosh's creed: take money where you find it.
dowry — what does it mean?
Money or goods a bride brings to a marriage. These are the coins that would be Leah's dowry.
