📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
The Bronze Bow
Elizabeth George Speare · Chapters 9–16 · read from your own copy
You are back at your Daniel's Choices board, the walk-around whiteboard you started in Week 1. This week you gather the choices Daniel makes in Chapters 9 through 16. These are the chapters where he sets up in the village smithy, takes care of his sister Leah, and the village band forms around him. Watch for choices that seem to pull in two directions at once. Everything you gather feeds your essay "The Power of Choice: Daniel's Journey."
You are not visiting a ready-made world. You are adding to a board of your own evidence, using moments you found in the reading.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
In these chapters, where do Daniel's choices start to pull away from hatred?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place notes and simple objects. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your Daniel's Choices board from Week 1, your own copy of the book, and your Reading Journal open to your Week 2 notes.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open your Daniel's Choices board. Add a new section titled Week 2 (Chapters 9–16) so this week's notes stay together.
2
Make a note for this week's question: where do Daniel's choices begin to pull away from hatred? Write it in one sentence.
3
Make four to six choice notes, one moment each, from Chapters 9 through 16: Daniel sparing the old miser and giving back his dagger instead of robbing him; staying the night with his dying grandmother and reciting Psalm 23 while Leah reaches for his hand; accepting Simon's smithy and house so he can support Leah, and leaving the mountain; promising Simon he will serve even Roman customers civilly; taking in Nathan and Kemuel and forming the village band; forging the small bronze bow brooch as a token of his purpose. Use the moments you actually noticed.
4
Add one or two "what drives him" notes beside each choice. Watch for the two voices pulling at him: Rosh's "take the money where we find it" and the carpenter's "each one of you is precious in His sight." Naming what drives each choice builds your evidence for what is starting to change in Daniel.
5
Take a screenshot of your Week 2 section. You'll submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 5.2A: Brainstorm Board. (No headset? Build the same notes on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board has a Week 2 section that names this week's question and has four to six real choice moments from Chapters 9 through 16.
☐
I named one or two things driving each choice, including the two voices pulling at Daniel.
☐
I submitted a screenshot of my board to VR 5.2A.
