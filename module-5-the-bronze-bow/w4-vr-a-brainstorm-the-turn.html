📝 WHAT YOU ARE DOING IN VR THIS WEEK
Board the Moment the Hatred Lets Go
A brainstorm board on Chapter 24 and the bronze-bow symbol
You have finished the book. The biggest change in Daniel happens in a quiet room: Jesus comes to the house, Leah's fever breaks, the weight Daniel has carried lifts, and he crosses the road to invite the Roman soldier Marcus inside. This week you spread that turn out on a board so you can see every part of it at once. You will collect what changes, what stays, and how the bronze bow (the bow no one can bend by his own strength) finally bends. This is raw material for your essay, not the essay yet.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
In the quiet climax of Chapter 24, what exactly changes in Daniel, and how does the bronze bow show it?
💡 BEFORE YOU PUT ON YOUR HEADSET
Device: your VR headset (solo, at your own pace)
Tool you'll use: a blank board or open space where you can place and move notes. [Teacher: confirm the build/notes/recording tools students use in the current VR platform]
Bring with you: your running Daniel's Choices board from Weeks 1–3 (his choices laid out from hatred on one side to love on the other) and your Reading Journal notes on Chapter 24.
📝 WHAT TO DO, IN ORDER
1
Open a board. Across the top, place three notes for the three beats of the turn in Chapter 24: Jesus comes to the house, Leah is healed, and Daniel invites Marcus in. These are the steps of the climax, in order.
2
Under each beat, add one note on what changes inside Daniel at that moment. For example: the weight he has carried the whole book lifts, a peace he had never imagined arrives, and his hatred finally gives way. Pull the exact moments from your own copy of the chapter.
3
Make a bronze bow note off to the side. Speare uses the bow no man can bend alone as a symbol (an object that stands for an idea). In one sentence, write what the bronze bow stands for now that the book is over, and connect it to the turn you just boarded.
4
Now look back at your Daniel's Choices board from Weeks 1–3. Add the Chapter 24 turn to the love end of it, so the board now stretches from his vow of hatred all the way to the open door. You will finish that full board in VR 5.4B.
5
Take a screenshot of your brainstorm board (the three beats, what changes, and the bronze-bow note). Submit it in the Canvas assignment VR 5.4A: Brainstorm the Turn. (No headset? Lay the same notes out on paper or in a slide and submit a photo.)
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
My board shows the three beats of the turn in order, each with a note on what changes inside Daniel.
☐
I wrote one sentence on what the bronze bow stands for now, tied to the turn.
☐
I added the Chapter 24 turn to the love end of my Daniel's Choices board, then submitted a screenshot to VR 5.4A.
