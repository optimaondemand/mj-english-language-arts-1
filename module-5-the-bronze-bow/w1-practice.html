Answer each row about proper nouns and sentence types. Pick one and you'll get instant feedback.

⚡ VOCAB PRACTICE · TAP EACH CARD TO CHECK YOURSELF
Guess the meaning of each word first. Then tap the card to check.
📎 tetrarch (tap to flip)
A ruler over one part of a region, set up under the Roman Empire (the tetrarch's palace in Tiberias).
📎 legionaries (tap to flip)
Roman foot soldiers, the men who enforce Rome's rule.
📎 synagogue (tap to flip)
A building where Jewish people gather to pray and hear the Scriptures read.
📎 manacles (tap to flip)
Iron rings and chains locked on the wrists to hold a prisoner or slave.
📎 Zealot (tap to flip)
A member of the movement that wanted to drive Rome out of Israel by force.
📎 vow (tap to flip)
A solemn, serious promise (Daniel's vow against Rome).
