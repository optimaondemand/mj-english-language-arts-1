# M/J English Language Arts 1 — 6th Grade ELA (ELA6)

Interactive, self-contained lesson pages for Optima Academy Online's 6th Grade ELA course,
Semester 1 (Modules 1–3). Hosted here via GitHub Pages and embedded into Canvas lesson pages
via iframe. These pages are **ungraded scaffolding** — all graded items (quizzes, assignments,
discussions) are built natively in Canvas and are not part of this repo.

## Structure
- `module-1-st-george-and-the-dragon/` — *St. George and the Dragon* (Michael Lotti), 6 weeks
- `module-2-poetry-and-short-stories/` — Nash, Thomas, Hawthorne (+ Shakespeare Sonnet 18), 4 weeks
- `module-3-king-arthur/` — *King Arthur and His Knights* (Roger Lancelyn Green), 6 weeks
- `_shared/` — Course Welcome, Digital Binder Setup, Reading Journal in M365, M365 Setup Guide

## Copyright
Anchor novels (*St. George and the Dragon*, *King Arthur and His Knights*) are **not public
domain**. No full text, PDF, or ebook of any anchor novel is hosted in this repo — pages teach
*about* the texts, with original instruction and only brief fair-use excerpts. Students read
from their own lawful copy.
